Canonical Scholarship Verification 
Report: UniFAST and CHED Priority 
Programs 
Systemic verification of higher education funding programs requires rigorous alignment with Tier 
1 primary evidence, including government decrees, joint memorandum circulars, and official 
provider guidelines. Operational scholarship matching engines depend on deterministic 
validation to eliminate false-positive recommendations that misdirect applicants and 
false-negative omissions that suppress viable funding opportunities. 
This canonical verification audit evaluates five higher education financial assistance programs 
administered by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the Unified Student 
Financial Assistance System for Tertiary Education (UniFAST): the Tulong Dunong Program (ID 
5), the Tertiary Education Subsidy (ID 66), the SIDA Scholarship Grant Program (ID 118), the 
Estatistikolar Program (ID 119), and the SIKAP Faculty Grant (ID 120). 
Tulong Dunong Program (TDP) (ID: 5) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: CHED-UniFAST Tulong Dunong Program (CHED-TDP / UniFAST-TDP) 
●​ Provider: Unified Student Financial Assistance System for Tertiary Education (UniFAST) / 
Commission on Higher Education (CHED) 
●​ Category: Government / National / Undergraduate / Need-based Grant-in-Aid 
●​ Official Website: https://unifast.gov.ph 
●​ Application Portal: https://unifast.gov.ph/tes.html 
●​ Official Guidelines: CHED-DBM Joint Memorandum Circular (JMC) No. 04, s. 2019; 
UniFAST Memorandum Circular No. 02, s. 2026 
●​ Current Status: Active 
2. Purpose 
The CHED-UniFAST Tulong Dunong Program provides targeted financial assistance to qualified 
and deserving Filipino tertiary students enrolled in public or private higher education institutions. 
Designed as a grant-in-aid initiative under the Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES) umbrella 
framework, the program supplements the direct and indirect costs of higher education, including 
living allowances, books, and essential educational expenses. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Filipino citizen 
●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Education Level: Undergraduate / College 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th Year 

●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes 
●​ Graduate Students?: No 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must be enrolled in a first undergraduate degree in 
State Universities and Colleges (SUCs), CHED-recognized Local Universities and 
Colleges (LUCs) with Certificate of Program Compliance (COPC), or Private Higher 
Education Institutions (HEIs) with COPC or listed in the CHED Registry 
●​ Academic Requirements: Senior High School report card / Form 138 (for incoming 
freshmen) or a certified true copy of grades for the latest semester attended (for ongoing 
college students) 
●​ Minimum GWA: 75.00% (passing grade) 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Income Ceilings: Combined annual gross family income of parents or legal guardians 
must not exceed ₱400,000.00 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted to SUCs, CHED-recognized LUCs with COPC, or Private 
HEIs with COPC / included in the CHED Registry of Programs 
●​ Priority Courses: Any recognized undergraduate degree program 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Good Moral: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Other Official Rules: Applicants must not be availing of multiple national 
government-funded educational grants, except for Free Higher Education under Republic 
Act 10931, DSWD AICS, or Student Monetary Assistance for Recovery and Transition 
(SMART) 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Graduating high school students, incoming college freshmen, and 
ongoing undergraduate college students 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes (grantees must reapply or re-confirm qualification 
annually per academic cycle) 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Varies by CHED Regional Office (CHEDRO) and partner institution 
schedule 
●​ Closing Date: September 30 per JMC guidelines / as announced by regional advisories 
●​ Application Cycle: Annual (disbursed semestrally at ₱7,500.00 per term) 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2024–2025 / AY 2025–2026 
6. Benefits 

●​ Tuition: Included in the financial grant (up to ₱15,000.00 per AY; serves as a direct 
subsidy in SUCs/LUCs where tuition is covered under RA 10931) 
●​ Monthly Stipend: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Integrated into general living 
allowance) 
●​ Allowance: ₱15,000.00 per academic year (₱7,500.00 per semester) 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into general educational grant 
●​ Transportation: Integrated into living subsidy 
●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Return Service: None 
●​ Other Benefits: Direct educational grant-in-aid supplementing Free Higher Education 
7. Required Documents 
1. Fully accomplished UniFAST-TDP Application Form (Annex 2) 2. Certificate of Enrollment 
(COE) or Certificate of Registration (COR) 3. Certificate of Indigency issued by the Barangay, 
latest BIR Form 2316 / Income Tax Return, BIR Certificate of Tax Exemption, or Social Case 
Study Report 4. Academic Record: Form 138 / SF9 for incoming freshmen, or Certified True 
Copy of Grades for the latest semester attended for ongoing students 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Maintain a passing General Weighted Average (GWA) of at least 75.00% 
●​ Regular Load: Carry a regular academic credit load per semester as determined by the 
HEI 
●​ No Failures: Maintain passing standing across all enrolled subjects 
●​ Return Service: None 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Concurrent enjoyment of major national government educational scholarship grants 
(excluding RA 10931 Free Tuition and DSWD AICS) 
●​ Combined annual family gross income exceeding ₱400,000.00 
●​ Enrollment in non-COPC degree programs or non-recognized private institutions 
●​ Failure to maintain a passing GWA or regular credit load

CHED-UniFAST Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES) (ID: 
66) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: CHED-UniFAST Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES) (enacted under 
Republic Act No. 10931, Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act) 
●​ Provider: Unified Student Financial Assistance System for Tertiary Education (UniFAST) / 
Commission on Higher Education (CHED) 
●​ Category: Government / National / Undergraduate / Need-based Subsidy 
●​ Official Website: https://unifast.gov.ph 
●​ Application Portal: UniFAST Portal / HEI-administered Portal 
●​ Official Guidelines: CHED-UniFAST-DBM Joint Memorandum Circular No. 01 s. 2017; 
JMC No. 04 s. 2020; Memorandum Circular No. 1 s. 2021 (TES-3B) 
●​ Current Status: Active 

2. Purpose 
The Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES) provides comprehensive financial support to qualified, 
socioeconomically disadvantaged Filipino students pursuing undergraduate degrees in SUCs, 
LUCs, and private HEIs. Established under RA 10931, the subsidy covers tuition and 
matriculation in private institutions (TES-1), living allowances and learning materials (TES-2), 
additional allowances for students with disabilities (TES-3A), and licensure examination 
reimbursements (TES-3B). 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Filipino citizen 
●​ Residency: Priority given to students residing and studying in municipalities without public 
university campuses 
●​ Education Level: Undergraduate / College 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th Year 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes 
●​ Graduate Students?: No 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must be enrolled in an undergraduate degree program 
in an SUC, CHED-recognized LUC, or private HEI included in the UniFAST Registry 
●​ Academic Requirements: Passing GWA and regular academic credit load per term 
●​ Minimum GWA: 75.00% (passing grade per HEI retention standards) 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Income Ceilings: Dynamically evaluated based on DSWD Listahan 2.0 / 4Ps household 
income ranking and poverty threshold deciles 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ School Restrictions: SUCs, LUCs with institutional recognition, or private HEIs in the 
official UniFAST Registry 
●​ Priority Courses: Any recognized undergraduate degree program 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: Priority given to 4Ps / Listahan households, Persons with 
Disabilities (TES-3A), and students in municipalities without public SUC/LUC campuses 
●​ Good Moral: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Other Official Rules: Evaluated via UniFAST prioritization hierarchy: (1) Continuing 
StuFAPs grantees, (2) Listahan 2.0 / 4Ps ranked households, (3) Municipality exclusivity 
applicants 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Enrolled undergraduate students submitted by their respective HEIs 
during official UniFAST portal intake calls 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No (except claiming TES-3B licensure reimbursement within 2 

years of graduation) 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes (continuing grantees are re-validated semestrally) 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Set by UniFAST per academic billing cycle 
●​ Closing Date: Specified in UniFAST regional advisories 
●​ Application Cycle: Annual prioritization / Semestral disbursement 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2024–2025 / AY 2025–2026 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: TES-1 covers full tuition and school fees in private HEIs (up to ₱20,000.00/sem or 
₱40,000.00/AY) 
●​ Monthly Stipend: Integrated into living allowance (TES-2) 
●​ Allowance: TES-2 living allowance: up to ₱40,000.00 per AY (₱20,000.00 per semester) 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into TES-2 allowance 
●​ Transportation: Integrated into TES-2 allowance 
●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: TES-3B provides up to ₱10,000.00 one-time reimbursement for 
licensure review and examination fees 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Return Service: None 
●​ Other Benefits: TES-3A provides an additional ₱30,000.00 per AY for Persons with 
Disabilities (PWDs) 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Certificate of Enrollment / Registration Form (COR) from UniFAST-registered HEI 
2.​ DSWD 4Ps / Listahan Household ID, or Barangay Certificate of Indigency 
3.​ Valid Student ID / Government ID 
4.​ For TES-3A: PWD ID issued by NCDA / LGU 
5.​ For TES-3B: PRC Official Receipt, Review Center Receipt, and Notarized Letter of Intent 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Maintain a passing GWA per semester according to HEI retention rules 
●​ Regular Load: Enrolled in a regular credit load per term 
●​ No Failures: Compliance with academic standing requirements 
●​ Return Service: None 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Enrollment in non-registered private HEIs or non-compliant programs 
●​ Exceeding the maximum residency period of the degree program 
●​ Failure to maintain passing GWA or academic dismissal from the HEI 
●​ Possession of an earned post-secondary or college degree

Scholarship Grant Program for Children and 
Dependents of Sugarcane Industry Workers and Small 
Sugarcane Farmers (SIDA-SGP) (ID: 118) 

1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: Scholarship Grant Program for Children and Dependents of Sugarcane 
Industry Workers and Small Sugarcane Farmers (SIDA-SGP) 
●​ Provider: Commission on Higher Education (CHED) in partnership with the Sugar 
Regulatory Administration (SRA) under Republic Act No. 10659 (Sugarcane Industry 
Development Act of 2015) 
●​ Category: Government / National / Need-based / Sectoral Agriculture Grant 
●​ Official Website: https://legacy.ched.gov.ph/sida-sgp/ 
●​ Application Portal: SRA Mill District Offices / CHED Regional Office Portals 
●​ Official Guidelines: CHED Memorandum Order (CMO) No. 30, s. 2016; CMO No. 02, s. 
2020 (Amendments); CMO No. 15, s. 2026 
●​ Current Status: Active 
2. Purpose 
SIDA-SGP provides educational assistance to qualified children and dependents of sugarcane 
industry workers and small sugarcane farmers. Mandated by RA 10659, the program supports 
undergraduate and graduate studies in agriculture, agricultural engineering, chemical 
engineering, and sugar technology offered at State Universities and Colleges (SUCs) to build 
human capital for the Philippine sugarcane industry. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Filipino citizen 
●​ Residency: Resident of sugarcane-producing mill districts in the Philippines 
●​ Education Level: Undergraduate (College) and Graduate (Master's / Doctoral) 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Undergraduate: Years 1–4; Graduate: Years 1–3 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes (with earned units relevant to priority degree programs) 
●​ Graduate Students?: Yes (Master's and Doctoral degree levels) 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Enrolled or accepted in an identified State University 
and College (SUC) 
●​ Academic Requirements: SHS report card / Form 138 (for freshmen), certified true copy 
of grades for latest semester (for college), or TOR & Diploma (for graduate applicants) 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Selection ranked 70% 
Academic + 30% Income; passing GWA required for retention) 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Income Ceilings: Combined annual gross income of parents/guardian must not exceed 
₱400,000.00 for Undergraduate track; combined annual gross income of 
applicant/spouse/parents must not exceed ₱500,000.00 for Graduate track 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted strictly to identified State Universities and Colleges 
(SUCs) 
●​ Priority Courses: Agriculture, Agricultural Engineering and Mechanics, Chemical 
Engineering, Sugar Technology, and related ladderized programs specified under Sec. 
6(b) of RA 10659 

●​ Sectoral Requirements: Must be certified by the Sugar Regulatory Administration (SRA) 
as legitimate children or dependents of sugarcane industry workers or small sugarcane 
farmers 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Certificate of Good Moral Character) 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Other Official Rules: Applicants must submit complete documents to their local SRA Mill 
District office to secure SRA certification prior to CHEDRO ranking 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Graduating SHS students, ongoing college students with earned units, 
and graduate applicants admitted to SUCs 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes 
●​ Can graduates apply?: Yes (for Master's/Doctoral programs) 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Set annually per CHEDRO and SRA advisory 
●​ Closing Date: Announced per academic cycle 
●​ Application Cycle: Annual 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–2027 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: SUC tuition is free under RA 10931 for undergraduates; Graduate track provides 
up to ₱60,000.00 per AY (₱30,000.00 per semester) for TOSF 
●​ Monthly Stipend: ₱10,000.00 per month (₱100,000.00 per 10-month AY) for both 
Undergraduate and Graduate tracks 
●​ Allowance: Integrated into monthly stipend 
●​ Book Allowance: ₱10,000.00 per AY (₱5,000.00 per semester) 
●​ Transportation: Integrated into monthly stipend package 
●​ Insurance: Medical insurance integrated into stipend package 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Thesis proposal required 
for graduate track entry) 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Return Service: Mandatory 1 year of return service in the Philippines for every 1 year of 
scholarship availed, completed within 2 years after graduation (prioritizing government 
agencies directly working with the sugarcane industry, other government offices, or 
related private entities) 
●​ Other Benefits: Total financial package value: ₱110,000.00/AY for Undergraduate track; 
₱175,000.00/AY for Graduate track 

7. Required Documents 
1.​ Official SRA Certification confirming applicant as child/dependent of a sugarcane worker 
or small sugarcane farmer 
2.​ Certificate of Good Moral Character 
3.​ Notice of Admission / Certificate of Registration from participating SUC 
4.​ Academic Record: Form 138 / SF9 (for SHS), Certified True Copy of Grades for latest 
semester (for college), or TOR & Diploma (for graduate applicants) 
5.​ Proof of Income: Latest Income Tax Return (ITR) or BIR Certificate of Tax Exemption 
(Income

●​ Maintain GWA: Maintain passing GWA per semester as prescribed by SUC retention 
policies 
●​ Regular Load: Full-time credit load in approved priority degree program 
●​ No Failures: Maintain regular academic standing without failing grades 
●​ Return Service: Signed commitment to render 1:1 return service post-graduation 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Absence of official Sugar Regulatory Administration (SRA) certification 
●​ Combined gross family income exceeding ₱400,000.00 (undergraduate) or ₱500,000.00

13. Matching Risks 
●​ Missing Sectoral Restriction Risk: The live database lacks an explicit tag for SRA 
certification. The matching engine must validate user.is_sra_certified_dependent == true

Systemic Directives and Production Database Updates 
Secondary and third-order policy analysis demonstrates three vital structural requirements for 
database engineering: 
1.​ Sectoral Grants Depend on External Agency Registries: Programs such as SIDA-SGP (ID 
118) require verification against external government agency rosters (Sugar Regulatory 
Administration mill district rosters). Production databases must store explicit sectoral tags 
(sectoral_restriction) to avoid qualifying general agriculture students based solely on 
course title. 
2.​ Dual-GWA Architectures Require Temporal Evaluation: Programs like Estatistikolar (ID 
119) enforce distinct GWA thresholds depending on academic standing: 85.00% for 
incoming Grade 12 graduates and 80.00% for ongoing 2nd to 4th-year college students. 
Engine logic must branch dynamically based on user.year_level rather than evaluating a 
single static GWA column. 
3.​ Institutional Capacity Tags Prevent Disqualification: SIKAP (ID 120) requires enrollment in 
designated Delivering Higher Education Institutions (DHEIs), whereas TDP (ID 5) requires 
COPC accreditation. Production matching algorithms must validate 

school.accreditation_tags against scholarship requirements prior to output generation. 
Executing these database structural updates aligns the production system with canonical 
mandates, eliminating matching errors and delivering reliable recommendations to Filipino 
applicants.
