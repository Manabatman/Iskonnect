Taguig LANI Premier Scholarship (ID: 27) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: Taguig City Lifeline Assistance for Neighbors In-Need (L.A.N.I.) Premier 
Scholarship1 
●​ Provider: Taguig City Government / Taguig Scholarship Secretariat1 
●​ Category: Local Government Unit (LGU) / Merit-based1 
●​ Official Website: https://www.taguig.gov.ph1 
●​ Application Portal: https://tcu.edu.ph/lani-scholarship1 
●​ Official Guidelines: Taguig City Ordinance No. 9, Series of 2011; Executive Order No. 
2011-111 
●​ Current Status: Active3 
2. Purpose 
To provide financial assistance and merit incentives to qualified Taguig City residents pursuing 
higher education in top-tier higher education institutions, specifically the University of the 
Philippines System (Luzon campuses) and private/public higher education institutions in the 
National Capital Region (NCR) recognized as Centers of Excellence by the Commission on 
Higher Education (CHED)1. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Filipino Citizen1 
●​ Residency: Bona fide resident of Taguig City for at least three (3) years immediately 
preceding the application1. 
●​ Education Level: College / Undergraduate1. 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: 1, 2, 3, 4, 51. 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No1. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes1. 
●​ Graduate Students?: No (Covered under LEAD track ID 97)1. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Enrolled or enrolling in the UP System (Luzon 

campuses) or CHED-certified Centers of Excellence in NCR1. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Senior High School or college academic performance meeting 
admission and retention cutoffs1. 
●​ Minimum GWA: 90% (or equivalent 1.75 semestral weighted average)1. 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Income Ceilings: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted to University of the Philippines System (Luzon campuses) 
and CHED-certified Centers of Excellence in NCR1. 
●​ Priority Courses: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Certificate of Good Moral Character issued for the current 
school year)1. 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Other Official Rules: Must be a registered voter of Taguig City if 18 years or older, with at 
least one parent registered as a voter of Taguig City1. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Graduating SHS students and ongoing college students enrolled in 
qualifying COE institutions or UP Luzon campuses1. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes1. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes1. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes1. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes1. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No1. 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes (Requires semestral renewal)1. 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Semestral schedule announced by the Taguig Scholarship Secretariat1. 
●​ Closing Date: Announced per semestral intake window1. 
●​ Application Cycle: Semestral3. 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–20273. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: Integrated into financial grant1. 
●​ Monthly Stipend: Integrated into semestral allowance1. 
●​ Allowance: PHP 40,000.00 to PHP 50,000.00 per school year (PHP 20,000.00 to PHP 
25,000.00 per semester)1. 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into semestral allowance1. 
●​ Transportation: Integrated into semestral allowance1. 

●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Return Service: None mandated; scholars are encouraged to serve Taguig City1. 
●​ Other Benefits: Merit incentives awarded to scholars obtaining a semestral weighted 
average of 1.75 or higher1. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Completely filled-out LANI Scholarship Application Form with recent 2x2 pictures1. 
2.​ Registration Form or Official Receipt of Enrollment for the current semester1. 
3.​ Authenticated Copy of Grades/Transcript of Records for the preceding semester1. 
4.​ Certificate of Good Moral Character issued for the current academic year1. 
5.​ Voter's Certification issued by COMELEC (for applicant if >=18 years old, and parent)1. 
6.​ Certificate of Residency (minimum 3 years)1. 
7.​ Proof of Billing under the applicant's or parent's name1. 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Must maintain a General Weighted Average (GWA) not lower than 2.50 
per semester5. 
●​ Regular Load: Enrolled in at least 15 academic units per semester or equivalent per 
trimester5. 
●​ No Failures: Zero failing grades (5.0), unremoved 4.0, Incomplete (INC), or Dropped 
marks5. 
●​ Return Service: None1. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Residing in Taguig City for less than three (3) consecutive years1. 
●​ Non-voter status of applicant (if 18+) or parent in Taguig City1. 
●​ Semester GWA dropping below 2.50 or receiving grades of 5.0, 4.0, INC, or Dropped5. 
●​ Enrollment in non-COE institutions or non-UP Luzon campuses1.
