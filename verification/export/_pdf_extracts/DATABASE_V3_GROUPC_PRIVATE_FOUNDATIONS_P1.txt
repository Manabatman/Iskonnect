Canonical Verification Report: Group B 
Private Foundation Scholarships for the 
ISKONNECT Matching Engine 
Executive Summary & Verification Protocol 
The ISKONNECT scholarship matching platform operates an automated recommendation 
engine designed to process student profile vectors against higher education financial assistance 
programs across public, institutional, and private sectors in the Philippines. To eliminate 
false-positive recommendations that direct applicants to ineligible programs and false-negative 
omissions that suppress viable funding, this report establishes the canonical verification ground 
truth for all Group B database records that have not yet undergone canonical auditing. 
Enterprise database updates require deterministic verification: every eligibility parameter must 
be validated against Tier 1 primary evidence, mapped to explicit SQL data types, and reconciled 
with governing agency mandates. Where an official provider document or governing policy does 
not explicitly state a requirement, platform protocol mandates designating the field as NOT 
SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE rather than introducing unverified assumptions. 
Data verification strictly follows a four-tiered authority hierarchy: 
●​ Tier 1 (Highest Authority): Direct government agency web portals (.gov.ph), official 
provider portals, downloadable policy guidelines, CHED Memorandum Orders (CMOs), 
GSIS Policy and Procedural Guidelines (PPGs), DOST Administrative Orders, and official 
application portals. 
●​ Tier 2 (Official Announcements): Press releases, downloadable application instructions, 
official FAQs, and direct advisories published by scholarship secretariats. 
●​ Tier 3 (Trusted Secondary Sources): University scholarship office notices and 
recognized educational registries used exclusively to locate primary documentation. Tier 3 
data cannot override Tier 1 or Tier 2 mandates. 
●​ Tier 4 (Unverified Sources): Blogs, student forums, social media aggregators, and 
unverified summaries. These sources are permanently excluded from live database 
updates. 
Per platform protocol, scholarship programs that have already received complete canonical 
verification reports (e.g., DOST-SEI Undergraduate and JLSS, CHED BPMSP, CHED CMSP, 
CHED MSRS, Megaworld Foundation, SM Foundation, GSIS GSSP, CHED CoScho, and 
Quezon City Scholarship Program) are assigned to Group A and are excluded from re-research. 
This report presents exhaustive canonical audits for all seven target private foundation 
scholarships under Group B. 
Ayala Foundation U-Go Scholar Grant (ID: 11) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: Ayala Foundation U-Go Scholar Grant (U-GO Scholar Grant) 
●​ Provider: Ayala Foundation, Inc. (in partnership with U-Go Global) 

●​ Category: Private / Foundation / Merit-and-Need / Female Empowerment 
●​ Official Website: https://ayalafoundation.org/programs/scholarships/ 
●​ Application Portal: Official Eligibility Form Portal (sent via email/QR upon eligibility 
screening) 
●​ Official Guidelines: Ayala Foundation & U-Go Grant Program Official Guidelines 
●​ Current Status: Active 
2. Purpose 
The Ayala Foundation U-Go Scholar Grant provides financial assistance and educational 
support to high-potential young women from low-income families enrolled in public or state 
universities and colleges in the Philippines, helping cover living, transportation, and school 
expenses to ensure college completion and promote gender equity in higher education. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Filipino citizen 
●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Education Level: College / Undergraduate 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: 1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year (and 4th Year if enrolled in a 5-year 
degree program) 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes 
●​ Graduate Students?: No 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Enrolled or will enroll in a public or state 
university/college (SUC/LUC) in the Philippines 
●​ Academic Requirements: Minimum Grade Point Average / General Weighted Average 
(GWA) of at least 85% with no failing grades; no disciplinary or administrative cases 
●​ Minimum GWA: 85.00% 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Income Ceilings: Demonstrated financial need (family income within low-income 
threshold, parent ITR, BIR Tax Exemption, or Indigency) 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted to public or state universities and colleges (SUCs/LUCs) in 
the Philippines 
●​ Priority Courses: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: Must be a female student 
●​ Good Moral: Required (must have no disciplinary or administrative cases) 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Other Official Rules: Must NOT have any existing scholarship grant 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Female Filipino incoming 1st-year, 2nd-year, or 3rd-year college 
students (and 4th-year students taking a 5-year course) enrolled in public or state 
universities. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes 

●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes (Only if taking a 5-year course and entering 4th year; 
strictly ineligible if expecting to graduate in the current AY). 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes, provided they meet all eligibility criteria and hold 
no other scholarship grants. 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: May 5, 2026 
●​ Closing Date: June 6, 2026 
●​ Application Cycle: Annual 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2026–2027 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Public/state universities are covered 
under Republic Act 10931; grant provides direct educational financial support). 
●​ Monthly Stipend: Integrated into annual financial assistance. 
●​ Allowance: Approximately PHP 40,000.00 annual financial assistance. 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into annual grant. 
●​ Transportation: Integrated into annual grant. 
●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Return Service: None. 
●​ Other Benefits: Leadership development programs, professional development training, 
mentoring, and scholar support network access. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Duly accomplished online application form. 
2.​ Current official Certificate of Registration / Enrollment signed by the registrar with wet 
signature. 
3.​ Latest copy of grades / Transcript of Records signed by the registrar with wet signature. 
4.​ Proof of financial need (Parents' or guardians' most recent Income Tax Return [ITR], BIR 
Tax Exemption Certificate, Barangay Certificate of Indigency, or OFW/seafarer 
contract/proof of income). 
5.​ Proof of college admission or Senior High School diploma (for incoming 1st-year 
students). 
6.​ Recent copy of electric or water bill (if available). 
7.​ Official Recommendation Letter (for shortlisted applicants). 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Maintain a minimum GWA of 85.00% or equivalent each academic term. 
●​ Regular Load: Full-time credit load per term in a public/state university. 

●​ No Failures: Zero failing grades. 
●​ Return Service: None. 
### 9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Male gender. 
●​ Possession or active enjoyment of any other scholarship grant. 
●​ Enrollment in a private higher education institution. 
●​ Students expecting to graduate during the current academic year. 
●​ GWA below 85.00% or presence of failing/incomplete grades. 
●​ History of administrative or disciplinary sanctions.

2. Purpose 
The BPI Foundation Pagpupugay Scholarship honors the heroic contributions of medical 
frontliners who contracted COVID-19 or passed away in the line of duty during the pandemic by 
providing full financial support for the tertiary education of their qualified next of kin. 
### 3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Filipino citizen 
●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Education Level: College / Undergraduate 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: 1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year, 4th Year, and 5th Year 

●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes 
●​ Graduate Students?: No 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Enrolled or applying to any 4-year or 5-year 
college/university program in BPI Foundation partner schools nationwide (or non-partner 
HEIs upon direct coordination) 
●​ Academic Requirements: General Weighted Average (GWA) of at least 85.00% or 
equivalent / prevailing university standards for the previous school year (incoming 1st 
year) or previous semester (incoming 2nd-5th year) 
●​ Minimum GWA: 85.00% 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Income Ceilings: Demonstrated financial need verified via parents'/guardian's Income Tax 
Return (ITR) 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ School Restrictions: Open to students in BPI Foundation partner universities (e.g., Ateneo 
de Manila, Mapua, Malayan Colleges, National Teachers College, University of Nueva 
Caceres) and accredited nationwide HEIs 
●​ Priority Courses: Any 4-year or 5-year undergraduate degree program 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: Must be a qualified next-of-kin of medical frontliners (doctors, 
nurses, medical technologists, community health workers, administrative/utility/support 
staff in healthcare facilities) who passed away or contracted COVID-19 in the line of duty. 
Priority order: children of married frontliners; next-of-kin up to 3rd degree consanguinity 
for single frontliners 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Certificate of Good Moral Character) 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Other Official Rules: Requires hospital endorsement letter, official affidavits of 
endorsement and no objection, and proof of frontliner COVID-19 medical/death status 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Qualified next-of-kin of eligible medical frontliners entering or currently 
enrolled in 1st to 5th year of college. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes (4th and 5th year undergraduate students). 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes (Scholarships are evaluated for annual renewal). 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Announced per annual cycle (typically Q2/Q3) 
●​ Closing Date: Announced annually (e.g., July 31 / extended to September) 
●​ Application Cycle: Annual 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–2027 
6. Benefits 

●​ Tuition: Up to PHP 100,000.00 per academic year covering tuition and matriculation fees 
(paid directly to partner HEI). 
●​ Monthly Stipend: Provided as monthly living subsidy for State University/College (SUC) 
scholars. 
●​ Allowance: Learning assistance allowance provided for SUC scholars. 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into learning assistance allowance. 
●​ Transportation: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Return Service: None. 
●​ Other Benefits: Annual renewal support through course completion.

2.​ Endorsement Letter signed by authorized representative of hospital/healthcare facility 
where frontliner was assigned. 
3.​ Affidavit of Endorsement and No Objection (from frontliner if living, or next-of-kin if 
deceased).

nephew/niece up to 3rd degree). 
5.​ PSA Marriage Certificate (for widow/widower or parents). 
6.​ PRC ID or License ID of the medical frontliner. 
7.​ Death Certificate of frontliner indicating COVID-19 cause of death OR Medical Certificate

8.​ Parents' or guardian's latest Income Tax Return (ITR). 
9.​ Official Transcript of Records / Copy of Grades showing minimum GWA of 85.00%. 
10.​Certificate of Good Moral Character.

●​ Maintain GWA: Maintain a minimum GWA of 85.00% or prevailing university standards

●​ Regular Load: Enrolled in full term credit load. 
●​ No Failures: Compliance with university retention policies. 
●​ Return Service: None.

●​ Semester GWA dropping below 85.00%.

Multi-Stage Engine Recommendation Pipeline Architecture 
The automated recommendation algorithm processes applicant vectors through three sequential 
filtering stages to guarantee data compliance: 
1.​ Stage 1: Hard Level, Temporal, and Gender Filtering 
○​ Verifies whether user.year_level is contained within 
scholarship.eligible_year_levels. 
○​ Enforces user.earned_college_units == 0 whenever 
scholarship.incoming_year_only is set to true. 
○​ Validates strict demographic and intake constraints, such as verifying user.gender 
== 'FEMALE' for the Ayala Foundation U-Go Grant (ID 11) or restricting entry strictly 
to user.year_level == 2 for the Aboitiz Future Leaders Scholarship (ID 75). 
2.​ Stage 2: Quantitative and Socioeconomic Eligibility Bounds 
○​ Evaluates user.gwa >= scholarship.minimum_gwa or checks whether 
user.class_rank <= scholarship.rank_cutoff_alternative (such as the Top 10% batch 
rank required for freshman applicants under GBF STEM ID 72). 
○​ Confirms user.family_income <= scholarship.income_limit (such as enforcing the 
PHP 250,000.00 ceiling for Gabay Guro ID 16 or PHP 500,000.00 for MBFI 
ACCESS ID 13). 
○​ Enforces program exclusivity rules by validating user.has_existing_scholarship == 
false for Ayala U-Go (ID 11) and user.has_corporate_foundation_grant == false for 
Aboitiz Brights (ID 75). 
3.​ Stage 3: Institutional, Sectoral, and Course Validation 
○​ Matches user.school_id against the partner_schools database table whenever 
scholarship.partner_school_restricted is set to true. 
○​ Verifies user.course_code against approved priority degree arrays (such as 
restricting Gabay Guro ID 16 strictly to BEED and BSED majors, or matching STEM 
disciplines for GBF ID 72). 
○​ Validates specific sectoral attributes, such as verifying next-of-kin documentation for 
COVID-19 medical frontliners under the BPI Foundation Pagpupugay Scholarship 
(ID 14). 
Executing these database updates and architectural pipeline enhancements aligns the live 
ISKONNECT scholarship database with canonical provider rules, eliminating false 
recommendations and providing Filipino students with reliable scholarship matching results.
