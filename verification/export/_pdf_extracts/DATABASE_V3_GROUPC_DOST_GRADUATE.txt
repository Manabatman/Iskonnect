Canonical Verification Report: DOST-SEI 
Graduate Scholarship Programs 
A systemic audit of the live scholarship database (public.scholarships, export timestamp 
2026-08-05) reveals structural inaccuracies, incomplete data models, and critical logic 
mismatches between live database parameters and official agency mandates governing 
graduate funding. In a production-grade scholarship matching engine, loose data typing and 
qualitative assumptions generate severe failure modes. False-positive recommendations direct 
ineligible applicants to submit applications for programs that will reject them, while 
false-negative omissions suppress viable funding opportunities for qualified candidates. 
This report provides canonical verification for five Department of Science and Technology - 
Science Education Institute (DOST-SEI) graduate scholarship programs: the master umbrella 
entry (ID 3) and its four component sub-tracks—Accelerated Science and Technology Human 
Resource Development Program (ASTHRDP, ID 133), Engineering Research and Development 
for Technology (ERDT, ID 134), Capacity Building Program in Science and Mathematics 
Education (CBPSME, ID 135), and Science and Technology Education for Regional 
Development (STRAND, ID 136). 
Data verification strictly follows a four-tiered authority hierarchy: Tier 1 comprises direct 
government web portals (.gov.ph), official provider systems (science-scholarships.ph), official 
downloadable PDF guidelines, and governing administrative orders. Tier 2 covers official press 
releases and advisories published directly by governing secretariats. Tier 3 includes trusted 
university scholarship notices used solely to locate primary sources, while Tier 4 unverified 
blogs and secondary aggregators are discarded. Fields lacking explicit mandates in Tier 1 
sources are designated as NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE.

DOST-SEI Graduate Scholarship Programs 
(ASTHRDP/CBPSME/ERDT/STRAND) (ID: 3) 

1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: DOST-SEI Graduate Scholarship Programs (Umbrella Entry covering 
ASTHRDP, CBPSME, ERDT, and STRAND) 
●​ Provider: Department of Science and Technology - Science Education Institute 
(DOST-SEI) 
●​ Category: Government / National / Graduate / Merit-based 
●​ Official Website: https://www.science-scholarships.ph/ 
●​ Application Portal: https://www.science-scholarships.ph/ 
●​ Official Guidelines: DOST-SEI Local Graduate Scholarship Guidelines & Application 
E-Forms 
●​ Current Status: Active 
2. Purpose 
To boost the production of high-level human resources needed for Research and Development 
(R&D) in Science and Technology (S&T), upgrade engineering research capability, improve the 
quality of science and mathematics education, and strengthen the faculty profile of regional 
universities across the Philippines. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Natural-born or naturalized Filipino citizen. 
●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Education Level: Graduate (Master's Degree and Doctoral/PhD Degree). 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: MS Program: Years 1 and 2; PhD Program: Years 1, 2, and 3 (up to 
Year 4 for straight-PhD track). 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No (Lateral entry ongoing graduate students with earned units 
are eligible). 
●​ Existing College Students?: Ineligible for undergraduate degree enrollment; applicants 
must possess a completed Bachelor's degree (for MS) or Master's degree (for PhD). 
●​ Graduate Students?: Yes (Incoming 1st year graduate students and regular ongoing 
MS/PhD students). 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must be admitted or enrolled as a regular graduate 
student in an approved priority S&T program at a DOST-SEI consortium member 
university. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Must meet the admission requirements for graduate studies at 
the accepting consortium university. 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Governed by host university 
graduate school admission and retention standards; live database entry of 88% is an 
unverified artifact). 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Income Ceilings: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Uncapped / No parental or 
personal income limit is imposed in official DOST graduate guidelines; live database cap 
of ₱500,000 is incorrect). 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted to designated DOST-SEI Graduate Scholarship 

Consortium Member Universities (e.g., NSC, ERDT, CBPSME, and STRAND consortia). 
●​ Priority Courses: DOST-SEI Priority S&T Fields (Natural Sciences, Engineering, Science 
& Mathematics Education, Agriculture, Computer Science, etc.). 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: Educator status for CBPSME; Regional HEI faculty status for 
STRAND. 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Validated via NBI Clearance or Certificate of Good Moral 
Character). 
●​ Health Requirements: Physically and mentally fit to study, as certified by a licensed 
physician. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Must be a full-time student; must NOT be employed or practicing a 
profession while on scholarship, or must secure an official approved leave of absence 
(LOA) from the employer; must not enjoy concurrent government or private scholarships; 
must execute a service contract. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Graduating college seniors entering graduate school, BS/BA degree 
holders applying for MS, MS degree holders applying for PhD, and regular ongoing 
MS/PhD students. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Ineligible for undergraduate freshmen; incoming 1st-year 
graduate students may apply. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Ineligible for undergraduate sophomores; ongoing 
2nd-year MS/PhD graduate students may apply. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Ineligible for undergraduate juniors. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Graduating college seniors entering an MS program upon 
graduation may apply. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: Yes (Bachelor's graduates for MS; Master's graduates for PhD). 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes. 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Announced per academic cycle by consortium universities (typically 
March/April for 1st Semester; September/October for 2nd Semester). 
●​ Closing Date: Varies by university (e.g., June 30 for 1st Semester intake). 
●​ Application Cycle: Semester / Term intake. 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–2027. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: Full actual tuition and other school fees. 
●​ Monthly Stipend: ₱30,000.00 per month for MS scholars; ₱38,000.00 per month for PhD 
scholars. 
●​ Allowance: Learning Materials and/or Connectivity Allowance of ₱20,000.00 per 
Academic Year. 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into the Learning Materials and Connectivity Allowance 
(₱20,000.00/AY). 
●​ Transportation: Actual one (1) economy class roundtrip fare per Academic Year (for 
scholars studying outside their home province). 

●​ Insurance: Full Group Personal Accident Insurance coverage. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Research Allowance: Student Research Support Fund (SRSF): Outright 
Thesis/Dissertation Allowance of ₱60,000.00 (MS) or ₱100,000.00 (PhD); Research 
Grant up to ₱114,000.00 (MS) or ₱253,000.00 (PhD); Dissemination Grant up to 
₱75,000.00 (MS) or ₱150,000.00 (PhD); Mentor's Fee of ₱36,000.00 (MS) or ₱72,000.00 
(PhD). 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (10% of thesis 
allowance is released upon submission of final hardbound copies and graduation 
requirements). 
●​ Return Service: Mandatory return service obligation in the Philippines on a full-time basis 
immediately after degree completion, equivalent to one (1) year of service for every year 
of scholarship availed. 
●​ Other Benefits: Career Incentive Program (CIP) eligibility upon graduation with Graduate 
Fellow positions (MS: ₱59,802/month; PhD: ₱82,098/month); MECO-TECO Sandwich 
Scholarship Program research exchange eligibility. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Duly accomplished DOST-SEI Graduate Scholarship Information Sheet / Application 
Form. 
2.​ PSA Birth Certificate (Photocopy). 
3.​ Certified True Copy of Official Transcript of Records (TOR) for BS (and MS if applying for 
PhD). 
4.​ Official Endorsement Forms from two (2) former college professors (for MS) or two (2) 
former MS professors (for PhD). 
5.​ Medical Certificate from a licensed physician stating the applicant is physically and 
mentally fit to study. 
6.​ Valid NBI Clearance. 
7.​ Official Letter of Admission with regular status from the Program Head of the accepting 
institution. 
8.​ Approved Program of Study (POS). 
9.​ If employed: Official recommendation and approved Leave of Absence (LOA) from the 
head of agency, or proof of resignation. 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Must maintain a satisfactory General Weighted Average (GWA/GPA) 
prescribed by the host graduate school each term. 
●​ Regular Load: Must carry a full academic load per semester based on the approved 
Program of Study (9–12 units regular term, 3–6 units midyear). 
●​ No Failures: Must have no failing, incomplete, or dropped grades in coursework. 
●​ Return Service: Execution of scholarship agreement binding scholar to return service. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Employment or professional practice while on scholarship without an approved official 
leave of absence. 

●​ Failure to maintain academic retention standards or receiving a failing grade in any 
graduate subject. 
●​ Concurrent enjoyment of another government or private scholarship covering the same 
degree program. 
●​ Failure to comply with the return service obligation or non-completion of the degree 
program without valid justification (requires full financial refund with interest).

13. Matching Risks 
●​ Corrupted Financial Parameters: Live database ID 3 sets max_income: 500000 and 
allowance_mo: 15000. The matching engine must remove the income cap (as DOST 
graduate grants are uncapped) and update stipend parameters to ₱30,000 (MS) and 
₱38,000 (PhD). 
●​ Umbrella Redundancy: ID 3 functions as a generic master record covering four 
individual programs. If an applicant searches specifically for ERDT (engineering) or 
CBPSME (education), matching queries must route to the specific child records (IDs 
133–136) rather than returning duplicate umbrella entries.

Accelerated Science and Technology Human 
Resource Development Program (ASTHRDP) (ID: 133) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: Accelerated Science and Technology Human Resource Development 
Program (ASTHRDP) 
●​ Provider: Department of Science and Technology - Science Education Institute 
(DOST-SEI) 
●​ Category: Government / National / Graduate / Merit-based 
●​ Official Website: https://asthrdp.science-scholarships.ph/ 
●​ Application Portal: https://asthrdp.science-scholarships.ph/ 
●​ Official Guidelines: DOST-SEI ASTHRDP-NSC Information Sheet & Scholar Handbook 
●​ Current Status: Active 
2. Purpose 
To accelerate the production of high-level human resources for Research and Development 
(R&D) in basic and applied S&T fields, improve national global competitiveness, and support 
advanced scientific research in priority areas such as materials science, biotechnology, climate 
change, and drug development. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 

●​ Citizenship: Natural-born or naturalized Filipino citizen. 
●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Education Level: Graduate (Master of Science / PhD in S&T). 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: MS Track: Years 1 and 2; PhD Track: Years 1, 2, and 3 (up to 4 
years for straight-PhD). 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No (Lateral entry graduate students with earned units are 
eligible). 
●​ Existing College Students?: Ineligible for undergraduate degree; must possess a 
completed Bachelor's degree (for MS) or Master's degree (for PhD). 
●​ Graduate Students?: Yes (Incoming 1st year graduate students and regular ongoing 
MS/PhD students). 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must be admitted or enrolled in an approved MS/PhD 
program at a National Science Consortium (NSC) member university. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Must meet the graduate school admission standards of the host 
NSC member university. 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Governed by host NSC 
university graduate school retention policies). 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Income Ceilings: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Uncapped / No income 
ceiling). 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted strictly to ASTHRDP National Science Consortium (NSC) 
Member Universities: UP Diliman, UP Los Baños, UP Manila, UP Visayas, Ateneo de 
Manila University, De La Salle University, University of San Carlos, Mindanao State 
University - Iligan Institute of Technology, Central Luzon State University, and Visayas 
State University. 
●​ Priority Courses: Basic and Applied Sciences: Chemistry, Physics, Biological Sciences, 
Environmental Science, Meteorology/Earth Sciences, Mathematics, Computer Science, 
Materials Science, Food Science, Agriculture, and Biotechnology. 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Validated via valid NBI Clearance). 
●​ Health Requirements: Physically and mentally fit to undergo graduate studies, as certified 
by a licensed physician. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Must pursue full-time graduate studies; must NOT be employed or 
practicing a profession while on scholarship unless an official approved leave of absence 
(LOA) is granted; must execute a scholarship agreement. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Graduating college seniors entering graduate school, BS graduates 
applying for MS, MS graduates applying for PhD, and ongoing MS/PhD students at NSC 
universities. - Can current freshmen apply?: Ineligible for undergraduate freshmen; 
incoming 1st-year graduate students may apply. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Ineligible for undergraduate sophomores; ongoing 
2nd-year MS/PhD students may apply. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Ineligible for undergraduate juniors. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Graduating undergraduate seniors entering an MS program 
may apply. 

●​ Can graduates apply?: Yes (BS/BA graduates for MS; MS graduates for PhD). 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes. 
5. Application Window 
- Opening Date: Set annually per semester by NSC member universities (e.g., April/May for 1st 
Semester; September/October for 2nd Semester). 
●​ Closing Date: Varies by NSC university (e.g., July 31 for CLSU; August 15 for DLSU). 
●​ Application Cycle: Semester / Term intake. 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–2027. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: Full actual tuition and other school fees. 
●​ Monthly Stipend: ₱30,000.00 per month for MS scholars; ₱38,000.00 per month for PhD 
scholars. 
●​ Allowance: Learning Materials and/or Connectivity Allowance of ₱20,000.00 per 
Academic Year. 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into Learning Materials and Connectivity Allowance 
(₱20,000.00/AY). 
●​ Transportation: Actual one (1) economy class roundtrip fare per Academic Year (for 
scholars studying outside their home province). 
●​ Insurance: Full Group Personal Accident Insurance coverage. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Research Allowance: Student Research Support Fund (SRSF): Outright 
Thesis/Dissertation Allowance of ₱60,000.00 (MS) or ₱100,000.00 (PhD); Research 
Grant up to ₱114,000.00 (MS) or ₱253,000.00 (PhD); Dissemination Grant up to 
₱75,000.00 (MS) or ₱150,000.00 (PhD); Mentor's Fee of ₱36,000.00 (MS) or ₱72,000.00 
(PhD). 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (10% remaining thesis 
grant released upon hardbound submission). 
●​ Return Service: Mandatory service obligation in the Philippines on a full-time basis 
immediately after degree completion, equivalent to one (1) year for every year of 
scholarship enjoyed. 
●​ Other Benefits: Career Incentive Program (CIP) deployment as Graduate Fellow 
post-graduation; MECO-TECO Sandwich Program research exchange eligibility. 
7. Required Documents 
1. Accomplished ASTHRDP-NSC Information Sheet / Application Form. 2. Birth Certificate (PSA 
Photocopy). 3. Certified True Copy of Official Transcript of Records (TOR). 4. Endorsement from 
two (2) former college professors (for MS) or two (2) former MS professors (for PhD). 5. Medical 
Certificate from a licensed physician stating applicant is fit to study. 6. Valid NBI Clearance. 7. 
Official Letter of Admission with regular status from the accepting NSC university. 8. Approved 
Program of Study. 9. If employed: Recommendation and approved LOA from head of agency, or 
proof of resignation. 

8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Maintain required cumulative GPA/GWA set by the host NSC university. 
●​ Regular Load: Full-time academic load (9–12 units per regular semester, 3–6 units 
midyear). 
●​ No Failures: No failing or dropped grades in graduate coursework. 
●​ Return Service: Compliance with post-graduation return service contract. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Unapproved employment or practice of profession while holding the scholarship. 
●​ Academic failure or dropping below the host university's required retention GPA. 
●​ Concurrent enjoyment of another government or private scholarship. 
●​ Non-compliance with post-graduation service obligation (requires full financial refund with 
interest).

2. Purpose

mass of MS and PhD graduates in engineering, upgrade engineering research capability across 
academia and industry, and foster technological innovation for national industrial development. 

3. Official Eligibility Requirements

●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Education Level: Graduate (Master of Science in Engineering / PhD in Engineering). 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: MS Track: Years 1 and 2; PhD Track: Years 1, 2, and 3 (up to 4 
years for straight-PhD). 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No (Lateral entry ongoing graduate engineering students are 
eligible). 
●​ Existing College Students?: Ineligible for undergraduate degree; must possess a 
completed Bachelor's degree in engineering or related field. 
●​ Graduate Students?: Yes (Incoming 1st year graduate engineering students and regular 
ongoing MS/PhD engineering scholars). 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must be admitted or enrolled in an approved graduate 
engineering program at an ERDT Consortium member university. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Must satisfy the graduate school admission standards of the 
host ERDT consortium institution. 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Governed by host ERDT 
graduate school retention policies). 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Income Ceilings: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Uncapped / No income 
ceiling). 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted strictly to the eight (8) ERDT Consortium Member

Carlos, Central Luzon State University, and Mindanao State University - Iligan Institute of 
Technology.

Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Engineering, Electrical 
Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Energy Engineering, Environmental Engineering, 
Industrial Engineering, Materials Science & Engineering, Mechanical Engineering,

●​ Sectoral Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Validated via valid NBI Clearance). 
●​ Health Requirements: Physically and mentally fit to study, as certified by a licensed 
physician. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Must pursue full-time graduate engineering studies; must NOT be 
employed or practicing a profession while on scholarship unless an official approved leave 
of absence (LOA) is granted; must execute a scholarship agreement.

●​ Who May Apply: Graduating engineering seniors entering graduate school, BS 
engineering graduates applying for MS, MS engineering graduates applying for PhD, and 
ongoing graduate engineering students at ERDT universities. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Ineligible for undergraduate freshmen; incoming 1st-year 

graduate engineering students may apply. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Ineligible for undergraduate sophomores; ongoing 
2nd-year MS/PhD engineering students may apply. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Ineligible for undergraduate juniors. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Graduating engineering seniors entering an MS program 
upon graduation may apply. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: Yes (BS Engineering graduates for MS; MS Engineering 
graduates for PhD). 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes.

for 1st Semester; September/October for 2nd Semester). 
●​ Closing Date: Varies by ERDT university.

●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–2027.

●​ Monthly Stipend: ₱30,000.00 per month for MS scholars; ₱38,000.00 per month for PhD 
scholars.

Academic Year. 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into Learning Materials and Connectivity Allowance 
(₱20,000.00/AY).

scholars studying outside their home province). 
●​ Insurance: Full Group Personal Accident Insurance coverage. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE.

Grant up to ₱114,000.00 (MS) or ₱253,000.00 (PhD); Dissemination Grant up to 
₱75,000.00 (MS) or ₱150,000.00 (PhD); Mentor's Fee of ₱36,000.00 (MS) or ₱72,000.00 
(PhD). 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Return Service: Mandatory service obligation in the Philippines on a full-time basis 
immediately after degree completion, equivalent to one (1) year for every year of 
scholarship enjoyed. 
●​ Other Benefits: Career Incentive Program (CIP) deployment as Graduate Fellow 
post-graduation; MECO-TECO Sandwich Program research exchange eligibility.

4.​ Endorsement from two (2) former engineering professors. 
5.​ Medical Certificate from a licensed physician. 
6.​ Valid NBI Clearance. 
7.​ Official Letter of Admission with regular status from the accepting ERDT graduate school. 
8.​ Approved Program of Study. 
9.​ If employed: Recommendation and approved LOA from head of agency, or proof of 
resignation. 
8. Renewal Requirements

●​ Return Service: Compliance with post-graduation return service contract.

Multi-Stage Matching Engine Pipeline Architecture 
To eliminate matching errors, the live recommendation engine processes user vectors through a 
three-stage filtering pipeline: 
1.​ Stage 1: Hard Temporal and Academic Level Filtering 
○​ The engine verifies that user.education_level == 'Graduate' and user.earned_units 
>= 0. 
○​ It validates that user.year_level is contained within scholarship.eligible_year_levels. 
○​ It evaluates user.is_[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span)currently_enrolled 
against scholarship.requires_current_enrollment. 
2.​ Stage 2: Socio-Economic and Employment Validation 
○​ The pipeline checks employment status (user.is_employed): if the applicant is 
employed, it verifies that user.has_approved_loa == true. 
○​ It bypasses income ceiling checks for DOST graduate programs where 
income_limit is NULL. 
3.​ Stage 3: Institutional, Sectoral, and Program Alignment 
○​ The engine matches user.school_id against specific consortium member university 
tables (such as asthrdp_nsc_schools, erdt_consortium_schools, 
cbpsme_consortium_schools, or strand_consortium_schools). 
○​ It validates user.sectoral_status by checking teacher credentials for CBPSME (ID 
135) and regional HEI faculty status for STRAND (ID 136). 
○​ It confirms user.course_code against approved priority S&T graduate fields. 

Executing these database updates and implementing the multi-stage matching pipeline aligns
