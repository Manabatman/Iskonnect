Security Bank Foundation State Universities 
Scholarship (ID: 58) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: Security Bank Foundation State Universities Scholarship (Scholars for 
Better Communities Program) 
●​ Provider: Security Bank Foundation, Inc. (SBFI) 
●​ Category: Private / Corporate Foundation / Merit-and-Need 
●​ Official Website: https://www.securitybank.com/foundation 
●​ Application Portal: https://www.securitybank.com/foundation 
●​ Official Guidelines: SBFI Scholars for Better Communities Guidelines 
●​ Current Status: Active 
2. Purpose 
The Security Bank Foundation State Universities Scholarship is a corporate financial assistance 
and human capital development initiative established to support academically talented students 
entering designated public higher education institutions. By financing tertiary degrees in 
disciplines aligned with corporate operations, the foundation aims to expand access to quality 
education, build leadership capacity within state universities, and provide direct career pathways 
in the banking and finance sector. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Must be a natural-born or naturalized Filipino citizen. 
●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Education Level: Undergraduate / College. 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Year 1 (Incoming Freshmen) and limited upperclassmen in specific 
partner SUCs. 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: Primary focus is incoming freshmen; limited upperclassmen 
entry exists per SUC slot allocation. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes (limited slots at specific partner State Universities and 
Colleges). 
●​ Graduate Students?: Ineligible. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must be accepted or enrolled in a partner State 
University or College, such as Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP). 
●​ Academic Requirements: Must possess a Grade 12 General Weighted Average (GWA) of 

at least 93.00% or equivalent, with no subject grade lower than 86.00%, and grades of at 
least 90.00% in high school subjects aligned with the chosen college degree. 
●​ Minimum GWA: 93.00% (Entry GWA cutoff). 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Income Ceilings: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Requires documentary proof 
of financial status via Income Tax Return or Certificate of Indigency; live database records 
a PHP 350,000.00 ceiling). 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ School Restrictions: Strictly restricted to designated partner State Universities and 
Colleges. 
●​ Priority Courses: Degree programs aligned with bank operations, including Accountancy, 
Business Administration, Finance, Information Technology, Computer Science, Data 
Analytics, Communications, and Journalism. 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: Must NOT be a child or dependent of a Security Bank employee, 
and must not have an immediate family member within the second degree of 
consanguinity or affinity holding an active SBFI grant. 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Certificate of Good Moral Character from high school). - Health 
Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Must carry the full academic load prescribed by the university 
curriculum per term. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Graduating Senior High School Grade 12 students entering 1st year 
college and continuing 1st year SUC students. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: No (unless applying for designated continuing SUC 
slots). 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: No. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: No. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No. 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Announced annually (typically Q1/Q2 prior to the academic year opening). 
●​ Closing Date: Specified in annual call for applications. 
●​ Application Cycle: Fixed / Annual. 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–2027. 
### 6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: Full or partial tuition and matriculation fee coverage paid directly to the partner 
state university. 
●​ Monthly Stipend: Integrated into the annual educational grant package. 
●​ Allowance: Financial assistance package up to PHP 50,000.00 – PHP 60,000.00 per 
academic year. 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into the educational grant package. 
●​ Transportation: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 

●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Return Service: None mandatory; direct hiring opportunities provided upon graduation. 
●​ Other Benefits: Scholar development workshops, leadership training, internship 
placements, and corporate career placement assistance within Security Bank 
Corporation. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Duly accomplished SBFI Online Application Form. 
2.​ High School Transcript of Records / Grade 12 Report Card showing final GWA. 
3.​ Proof of Admission or Acceptance Letter from partner State University. 4. Proof of 
Financial Status (Latest Parent Income Tax Return, Certificate of Indigency, or OFW 
Contract). 
4.​ PSA Birth Certificate of student applicant. 6. Certificate of Good Moral Character. 
5.​ School Grading System documentation (if non-percentage system). 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Maintain a General Weighted Average (GWA) of at least 86.00% or 
equivalent by the end of each academic term. - Regular Load: Enrolled in the full unit load 
prescribed by the university curriculum. 
●​ No Failures: No grade lower than 80.00% (or equivalent) in any academic subject per 
term. 
●​ Return Service: None mandatory. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Being a child or dependent of an employee of Security Bank Corporation or its 
subsidiaries. 
●​ Having an immediate relative within the second degree of consanguinity or affinity holding 
an active SBFI scholarship. 
●​ Term GWA dropping below 86.00% or receiving a subject grade below 80.00%. 
●​ Enrolling in a non-partner state university or non-aligned degree program.

Formation Manual 
●​ Current Status: Active 
2. Purpose 
YSLEP serves as the flagship human development initiative of Caritas Manila, operating on the 
principle that education coupled with values formation provides an effective pathway out of 
systemic poverty. The program finances tertiary and technical-vocational education while 
instilling Christian values, civic responsibility, and servant leadership to transform scholars into 
productive, ethically grounded community leaders. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Must be a Filipino citizen. 
●​ Residency: Resident of Metro Manila (Archdiocese of Manila) or any of the 53 partner 
dioceses nationwide. 

●​ Education Level: Tertiary (College Undergraduate) and Technical-Vocational (TVET). 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Years 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5. 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes. 
●​ Graduate Students?: Ineligible. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Enrolled or accepted in an accredited university, 
college, or TVET institution. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Senior High School overall GWA of at least 85.00% or 
equivalent. 
●​ Minimum GWA: 85.00% (Entry and retention cutoff). 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Income Ceilings: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Requires a Certificate of 
Indigency from the Barangay or DSWD verifying financial distress; live database specifies 
PHP 180,000.00). 
●​ Age Restrictions: Must be between 18 and 25 years old at the time of application. 
●​ School Restrictions: Accredited partner institutions within participating dioceses. 
●​ Priority Courses: Open to all degree programs; includes a dedicated track 
(YSLEP-GEN129) prioritizing Agriculture degrees and sustainable farming. 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: Must be single; must be an active member of a Basic Ecclesial 
Community (GKK) or Parish Youth Ministry. 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Certificate of Good Moral Character). 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Zero-tolerance policy regarding vices (smoking, alcohol 
consumption, substance abuse, gambling, and computer game addiction); must NOT hold 
a Grant-in-Aid Working Student (GIA/WS) award from the school. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Single Senior High School graduates and continuing college or TVET 
students aged 18 to 25. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No. 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes. 
### 5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Set annually by local diocesan YSLEP secretariats. 
●​ Closing Date: Determined per participating diocese. 
●​ Application Cycle: Fixed / Annual. 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–2027. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: Direct financial grant covering matriculation and tuition fees. 
●​ Monthly Stipend: Integrated into the overall annual educational allowance. 
●​ Allowance: Annual financial support package valued at approximately PHP 30,000.00 – 
PHP 35,000.00 per scholar. 

●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into the annual grant package. 
●​ Transportation: Integrated into the annual grant package. 
●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Return Service: Post-graduation pledge ("Balik-Handog"): Join the Caritas YSL Alumni 
Association (CAMASA) and donate 1% of gross monthly salary (or a minimum of PHP 
100.00/month) upon employment to finance future scholars. 
●​ Other Benefits: Monthly Servant Leadership formation workshops, spiritual retreats, and 
community volunteerism programs. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Accomplished Caritas Manila YSLEP Application Form. 
2.​ Photocopy of SHS Report Card / College Transcript showing GWA \ge 85.00%. 
3.​ Certificate of Good Moral Character. 
4.​ PSA Birth Certificate or Baptismal Certificate. 
5.​ Certificate of Indigency from Barangay or DSWD. 
6.​ Parents' Marriage Certificate (photocopy). 
7.​ Two (2) 2x2 ID photos. 
8.​ Interview clearance from local Screening Committee. 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Maintain a general average of at least 85.00% per academic term. 
●​ Regular Load: Full-time credit load; shifting degree programs without approval is barred. 
●​ No Failures: Passing grades in all enrolled subjects. 
●​ Return Service: Must attend monthly YSL formation workshops and render 50 hours of 
volunteer work per school year in parish or community apostolates. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions

●​ Engagement in vices (smoking, alcohol, gambling, illegal drugs, or gaming addiction). 
●​ Holding a school Grant-in-Aid Working Student (GIA/WS) grant or major external award. 
●​ Term GWA dropping below 85.00% or unapproved shifting of degree program. 
●​ Failure to attend mandatory monthly YSL formation workshops or complete 50 annual 
volunteer hours.

●​ Provider: Armed Forces and Police Savings and Loan Association, Inc. (AFPSLAI) 
●​ Category: Private / Affiliation / Need-and-Merit 
●​ Official Website: https://www.afpslai.com.ph 
●​ Application Portal: Physical submission at nearest AFPSLAI branch office (forms 
downloadable online) 
●​ Official Guidelines: AFPSLAI Educational Grant Program (EGP) Policy Guidelines & Form 
EGP 
●​ Current Status: Active 
2. Purpose 
The AFPSLAI Educational Grant Program (EGP) is a corporate social responsibility initiative 
designed to assist active, retired, and deceased military and police personnel by subsidizing the 
tertiary education of their legal dependents. The program focuses on non-business degree 
programs to promote diverse professional development among military families. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Must be a Filipino citizen. 
●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Education Level: Undergraduate / College. 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Year 1 (Incoming Freshmen), Year 2 (Sophomores), and Year 3 
(Juniors). 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes (2nd and 3rd year college students; 4th year students are 

strictly barred). 
●​ Graduate Students?: Ineligible. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Accepted or enrolled in a non-business baccalaureate 
degree program. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Proof of highest educational attainment demonstrating passing 
academic standing. 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Passing academic standing 
required; live database records 85.00%). 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Income Ceilings: Total annual gross family income of the sponsor must not exceed PHP 
1,000,000.00. 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ School Restrictions: Recognized Philippine colleges and universities. 
●​ Priority Courses: Restricted EXCLUSIVELY to non-business baccalaureate courses (e.g., 
Computer Science, IT, Engineering, Social Sciences, Agriculture, Science). 
Business-related courses are covered under the separate Scholarship Apprentice 
Program). 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: Sponsor must be an active, retired, or deceased Regular Member 
of AFPSLAI in good standing. Applicant must be a legitimate, illegitimate, or legally 
adopted child. If sponsor is single/unmarried without children, a legitimate/adopted sibling 
may apply. 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Certificate of Good Moral Character). 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Strictly limited to ONE (1) grantee per sponsor/family; applicant must 
NOT hold any other external scholarship grant (except school merit tuition discounts). 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Dependents (children or qualified siblings) of regular AFPSLAI members 
who are incoming freshmen, 2nd-year, or 3rd-year college students. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: No (4th-year students are ineligible). 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No. 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: No (if another family member was a prior grantee). 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Announced annually (typically May/June). 
●​ Closing Date: Specified per cycle (e.g., June 26 or July 15). 
●​ Application Cycle: Fixed / Annual. - Current AY Covered: AY 2026–2027.

●​ Tuition: Up to PHP 30,000.00 per school term (and up to PHP 10,000.00 for mandatory 
summer/midyear terms). 
●​ Monthly Stipend: PHP 4,000.00 per month. 

●​ Allowance: ROTC incentive of PHP 2,000.00 per month during terms when ROTC is 
enrolled. 
●​ Book Allowance: Up to PHP 2,000.00 per school term (reimbursement basis). 
●​ Transportation: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Return Service: None required for EGP track (unlike the SAP business track which

●​ Other Benefits: Board and lodging subsidy up to PHP 2,000.00/month for SUC students 
when necessary.
