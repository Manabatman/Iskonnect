Canonical Verification Audit Report: 
ISKONNECT Scholarship Engine (Group 
B Program Audits) 
The ISKONNECT scholarship matching platform operates an automated engine designed to 
process student profile vectors against financial assistance programs across public, 
institutional, and private sectors1. To eliminate false-positive recommendations that direct 
applicants to ineligible programs and false-negative omissions that suppress viable funding, 
this canonical audit establishes the verified ground truth for all Group B database records1. 
Production database updates require deterministic verification: every eligibility parameter must 
be validated against Tier 1 primary evidence, mapped to explicit database columns, and 
reconciled with governing agency mandates1. Where an official provider document or 
governing policy does not explicitly state a requirement, platform protocol mandates 
designating the field as NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE rather than introducing 
unverified assumptions1. 
The data verification framework strictly adheres to a four-tiered authority hierarchy1. Tier 1 
(Highest Authority) comprises direct government agency web portals (.gov.ph), official 
provider portals, downloadable policy guidelines, CHED Memorandum Orders (CMOs), GSIS 
Policy and Procedural Guidelines (PPGs), DOST Administrative Orders, and official application 
portals1. Tier 2 covers official announcements, press releases, and direct advisories published 
by scholarship secretariats1. Tier 3 consists of trusted secondary sources, such as university 
scholarship office notices, which serve solely to locate primary documentation1. Tier 4 
comprises unverified aggregators, blogs, and student forums, which are permanently excluded 
from live database updates1. 
DepEd SHS Voucher Program (ID: 17) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: Senior High School Voucher Program (SHS VP)3 
●​ Provider: Department of Education (DepEd) in partnership with the Private Education 
Assistance Committee (PEAC)3 
●​ Category: Government / Basic Education Financial Assistance / Grant3 
●​ Official Website: https://deped.gov.ph5, https://ovp.peac.org.ph3 
●​ Application Portal: https://ovap.deped.gov.ph5 
●​ Official Guidelines: DepEd Order No. 11, s. 2015; DepEd Order No. 46, s. 2015; DepEd 
Order No. 60, s. 2017; DepEd Order No. 16, s. 20203 
●​ Current Status: Active 

2. Purpose 
The Senior High School Voucher Program is an institutionalized mechanism of financial 
assistance enacted under Republic Act No. 10533 (Enhanced Basic Education Act of 2013) and 
Republic Act No. 8545 (Expanded GASTPE Act)4. The program provides state subsidies in the 
form of non-cashable vouchers to qualified Grade 10 completers, enabling them to enroll in 
participating private senior high schools, State Universities and Colleges (SUCs), or Local 
Universities and Colleges (LUCs)3. By expanding student choice and leveraging private sector 
educational capacity through public-private partnerships, the voucher program guarantees the 
constitutional mandate of accessible, quality basic education while reducing overcrowding in 
public secondary schools5. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Natural-born or naturalized Filipino citizen5. 
●​ Residency: Resident of the Philippines5. 
●​ Education Level: Senior High School (Grades 11 and 12)4. 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Grade 11 (Initial redemption occurs upon entry into Grade 11)4. 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: Yes (Applicable to incoming Grade 11 SHS students)4. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Ineligible4. 
●​ Graduate Students?: Ineligible4. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must be enrolled or accepted in a non-DepEd 
VP-participating Senior High School3. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Successful completion of Grade 10 Junior High School (JHS)4. 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Passing Grade 10 completion is 
required)4. 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE5. 
●​ Income Ceilings: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Grade 10 completers from public 
JHSs and private JHS Educational Service Contracting [ESC] grantees are automatically 
qualified regardless of income; non-ESC private applicants undergo online screening 
subject to national budget allocations)5. 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE5. 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted to VP-participating non-DepEd Senior High Schools 
(Private SHSs, SUCs, and LUCs)3. 
●​ Priority Courses: All DepEd-approved SHS Tracks and Strands (Academic, 
Technical-Vocational-Livelihood [TVL], Sports, Arts and Design)4. 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: None5. 
●​ Good Moral: Certificate of Good Moral Character issued by the originating Junior High 
School4. 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Automatically Qualified Voucher Recipients (QVRs) include Grade 10 

completers from Public JHSs, SUC/LUC JHSs, and ESC grantees in Private JHSs5. 
Non-ESC private JHS completers must apply online via the Online Voucher Application 
Portal (OVAP)5. Vouchers are non-cashable grants disbursed directly to participating host 
schools via Land Bank of the Philippines accounts4. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Non-ESC Grade 10 completers from private junior high schools, and 
learners who completed JHS prior to the current school year re-entering basic 
education5. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes (Incoming Grade 11 SHS students)4. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: No (Grade 12 voucher renewal is automatic for Grade 
11 beneficiaries who maintain passing status)4. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: No4. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: No4. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No4. 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: No4. 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Announced annually via DepEd Order (typically opens between October 
and January)5. 
●​ Closing Date: Specified in the annual policy guidelines (typically February 28 for online 
submissions)5. 
●​ Application Cycle: Fixed / Annual5. 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–20275. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: Voucher subsidy disbursed directly to host school (NCR Private SHS: up to 
₱22,500/year; NCR SUC/LUC SHS: ₱17,500/year; Non-NCR Private SHS: up to 
₱17,500/year; Non-NCR SUC/LUC SHS: ₱14,000/year)4. 
●​ Monthly Stipend: None (Direct tuition voucher)5. 
●​ Allowance: None9. 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into the voucher value if published tuition fees are lower than 
the voucher cap4. 
●​ Transportation: None5. 
●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: Not Applicable4. 
●​ Research Allowance: None4. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: None4. 
●​ Return Service: None4. 
●​ Other Benefits: Voucher validity spans two consecutive years of continuous basic 

education (Grades 11 and 12)4. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ PSA Certified Birth Certificate4. 
2.​ Grade 10 Report Card (Form 138 / SF9) showing Learner Reference Number (LRN)4. 
3.​ Certificate of Junior High School Completion4. 
4.​ Certificate of Good Moral Character signed by JHS Principal4. 
5.​ ESC Certification Letter issued by JHS Principal via ESC IMS (for ESC grantees) or 
Qualified Voucher Applicant (QVA) Certificate (for online applicants)4. 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Promoted to Grade 12 in accordance with DepEd academic progression 
standards4. 
●​ Regular Load: Continuous full-time enrollment in the elected SHS track4. 
●​ No Failures: Passing grades across all enrolled SHS subjects4. 
●​ Return Service: None9. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Dropping out of Senior High School in the middle of an academic year4. 
●​ Transferring to a DepEd Public Senior High School4. 
●​ Failing Grade 11 or retaining Grade 11 academic status4. 
●​ Transferring to a non-participating private Senior High School3.

Senior High School graduates pursuing priority technical-vocational diploma programs offered 
by TESDA-registered Technical Vocational Institutions (TVIs)20. By supporting 
technical-vocational education in high-demand fields, the program bridges critical workforce 
gaps, addresses hard-to-fill occupations, and drives national industrial productivity20. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Natural-born or naturalized Filipino citizen24. 
●​ Residency: Resident of the Philippines24. 
●​ Education Level: Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TVET) Diploma 
level9. 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: 1st Year (Incoming first-time entrants into TVET diploma programs)20. 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: Yes20. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Ineligible (Must have earned zero college or tertiary units)21. 
●​ Graduate Students?: Ineligible21. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Accepted or enrolled in a priority diploma program 
offered by a TESDA-registered Technical Vocational Institution (TVI)20. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Grade 12 Senior High School General Weighted Average (GWA) 
of at least 90.00% or equivalent21. 
●​ Minimum GWA: 90.00%21. 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE for TVET track (HE track 
uses Top 5 / 95% GWA, TVET track mandates GWA >= 90%)21. 
●​ Income Ceilings: Combined annual gross income of parents/guardians must not exceed 
₱2,000,000.0020. 
●​ Age Restrictions: No age limit imposed (Irrespective of age provided candidate has 
earned no tertiary units)21. 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted to TESDA-registered Technical Vocational Institutions 
(TVIs) delivering approved priority diploma programs20. 
●​ Priority Courses: TESDA-identified priority diploma courses in key growth sectors20. 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: Special equity groups (PWDs, Solo Parents, IPs, Senior Citizens, 
first-generation students) receive 10 additional ranking points in selection scoring20. 
●​ Good Moral: Required21. 

●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Applicants must NOT previously hold a technical-vocational diploma 
or NC Level III or higher (unless acquired as part of the SHS curriculum)21. TESDA has sole 
authority and exclusive jurisdiction over the TVET Diploma Track21. Transferees or shiftees 
with credited tertiary units are ineligible26. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Graduating SHS Grade 12 students, prior SHS graduates with no tertiary 
units, and 4th year HS graduates from SY 2015–2016 or earlier with no tertiary units21. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes (Incoming 1st year TVET diploma students)21. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: No21. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: No21. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: No21. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: No21. 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: No21.

●​ Application Cycle: Fixed / Annual20.

●​ Allowance: Living stipend of ₱40,000.00 per academic year20.

●​ Research Allowance: None20. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: None20.

scholarship received, prioritizing public and government institutions20.

Recommendation Engine Architecture 
The completion of canonical audits across these Group B scholarship programs establishes a 
verified data foundation for the ISKONNECT scholarship matching platform1. By replacing 
unverified aggregator claims with official primary agency mandates, the system prevents 
misdirection and ensures accurate recommendations for Filipino students1. 

The recommendation engine processes student profiles through a multi-stage deterministic 
pipeline1: 
The initial stage conducts hard temporal and level filtering1. It verifies whether a student's 
education level and year level match scholarship.eligible_year_levels, enforces zero earned 
college units when scholarship.incoming_year_only is true (e.g., BPMSP TVET ID 77, OWWA 
CMWSP ID 87)21, and validates age restrictions (e.g., age under 21 for AFPEBSO ID 56)17. 
The second stage evaluates quantitative eligibility criteria1. It checks whether user.gwa >= 
scholarship.minimum_gwa (enforcing 80% for NCIP EAP ID 52 versus 85% for NCIP MBSP ID 
53)14, confirms user.family_income <= scholarship.income_limit (e.g., ₱200,000 for DA ACEF ID 
55 versus ₱2,000,000 for BPMSP ID 77)9, and validates specific salary caps (e.g., OFW parent 
monthly salary under USD $1,000 for OWWA ODSP ID 86)28. 
The third stage validates sectoral requirements, affiliation status, and institutional parameters1. 
It confirms tribal certifications (user.has_ncip_coc == true for NCIP ID 52 and 53)10, validates 
RSBSA agricultural registry status (user.is_rsbsa_dependent == true for DA ACEF ID 55)16, 
verifies active OWWA membership and casualty flags (user.parent_ofw_status IN ('Deceased', 
'Incapacitated') for OWWA ELAP ID 124)31, and restricts institutional grants (such as AFPEBSO ID 
56) to SUCs17. 
Implementing these database updates and pipeline constraints aligns the live ISKONNECT 
database with canonical provider rules, eliminating matching errors and delivering reliable 
scholarship recommendations1.
