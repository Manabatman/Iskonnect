international and bilateral scholarship programs available to Filipino applicants1. Developed for

immutable source of truth required for production database mutations and matching engine

distortion1. 
Taiwan ICDF Higher Education Scholarship Program 
(ID: 60) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: TaiwanICDF International Higher Education Scholarship Program3 
●​ Provider: Taiwan International Cooperation and Development Fund (TaiwanICDF)3 
●​ Category: Foreign Government / International / Merit-based / Graduate3 
●​ Official Website: https://www.icdf.org.tw3 
●​ Application Portal: https://www.icdf.org.tw / TaiwanICDF Online Application System3 
●​ Official Guidelines: TaiwanICDF International Higher Education Scholarship Program 
Application Guidebook4 
●​ Current Status: Active3 
2. Purpose 
To assist friendly and developing partner nations in human resource development by providing 
full financial support for talented international students to pursue Master's and Doctoral 
degrees across designated partner universities in Taiwan5. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Citizen of an eligible partner country, including the Philippines4. 
●​ Residency: Resident in the country of citizenship4. 

●​ Education Level: Graduate (Master's and Doctoral degree levels)3. 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Year 1 (Incoming Graduate Students)4. 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No4. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Ineligible for initial award unless applying for entry-level 
graduate degree studies4. 
●​ Graduate Students?: Yes3. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must apply for admission to a designated TaiwanICDF 
partner university program4. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Outstanding academic record from prior post-secondary 
studies4. 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Evaluated holistically via 
transcripts and university admission standards; live database parameter lists 85.00%)3. 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Income Ceilings: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Age Restrictions: Must be above 18 years of age4. 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted strictly to designated TaiwanICDF partner institutions4. 
●​ Priority Courses: Agriculture, Science and Engineering, Public Health and Medicine, 
Business Administration7. 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Good Moral: Good moral character required4. 
●​ Health Requirements: Satisfactory physical and mental health4. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Applicants cannot hold any other Republic of China (Taiwan) 
government scholarship concurrently8. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Bachelor's degree holders applying for Master's programs, and Master's 
degree holders applying for PhD programs at partner Taiwanese universities4. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: No 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: No 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: No 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes (provided they graduate prior to scholarship intake)8. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: Yes4. 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes4. 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Mid-January / February 1 annually8. 
●​ Closing Date: March 15 / March 31 annually4. 
●​ Application Cycle: Annual3. 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2026–20274. 

6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: 100% full coverage of tuition and credit fees3. 
●​ Monthly Stipend: NTD 15,000 for Master's students; NTD 17,000–18,000 for PhD 
students4. 
●​ Allowance: Campus housing / dormitory allowance provided4. 
●​ Book Allowance: Full textbook costs covered4. 
●​ Transportation: Economy class round-trip airfare4. 
●​ Insurance: Full health and accident insurance coverage4. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Research Allowance: Integrated into graduate institute research allocations4. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Return Service: None required by TaiwanICDF, though scholars are expected to return 
home to foster local development3. 
●​ Other Benefits: Pre-degree Mandarin language orientation8. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Completed Online Application Form4. 
2.​ Passport Biopage / Certificate of Nationality4. 
3.​ Highest Degree Diploma and Official Academic Transcripts4. 
4.​ Proof of English Language Proficiency (TOEFL / IELTS / Official Institutional Certificate)4. 
5.​ Two Letters of Recommendation4. 
6.​ Copy of Application Submission to a TaiwanICDF Partner University4. 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Satisfy academic GPA retention thresholds established by the host 
institution4. 
●​ Regular Load: Full-time credit load per semester4. 
●​ No Failures: Zero failing grades in enrolled coursework4. 
●​ Return Service: None3. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Holding ROC (Taiwan) citizenship or overseas Chinese student status4. 
●​ Concurrent enjoyment of another Taiwanese government scholarship8. 
●​ Failure to secure official admission from an approved TaiwanICDF partner university 
program4.

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters Scholarship (ID: 63) 

1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters Scholarship3 
●​ Provider: European Union / European Education and Culture Executive Agency (EACEA)3 
●​ Category: Foreign Government / International / Merit-based / Graduate3 
●​ Official Website: https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu3 
●​ Application Portal: 
https://www.eacea.ec.europa.eu/scholarships/erasmus-mundus-catalogue_en9 
●​ Official Guidelines: Erasmus+ Programme Guide (Key Action 2: Erasmus Mundus 
Action)11 
●​ Current Status: Active3 
2. Purpose 
To fund high-level, integrated transnational study programs at the Master level delivered by 
international university consortia, fostering academic excellence, international cooperation, and 
global student mobility across Europe and beyond9. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Open to candidates worldwide, including Filipino citizens10. 
●​ Residency: Worldwide residency; mandatory physical mobility across at least two 
different host countries11. 
●​ Education Level: Graduate (Master's degree level, 60, 90, or 120 ECTS)10. 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Incoming Master's students10. 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No10. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Graduating Bachelor's students eligible provided their degree 
is conferred prior to intake10. 
●​ Graduate Students?: Yes10. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must hold a recognized first higher education degree 
(Bachelor's degree or equivalent)10. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Outstanding academic performance in prior undergraduate 
studies10. 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Consortium-specific; live DB list is 
90.00%)3. 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Income Ceilings: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (No upper age limit). 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted to participating Erasmus Mundus Joint Master consortia 
HEIs9. 
●​ Priority Courses: Comprehensive academic fields listed in the Erasmus Mundus 
Catalogue9. 

●​ Sectoral Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Good Moral: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Health Requirements: Health insurance covered under EU framework10. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Mandatory physical mobility in at least two different countries11; 
maximum application limit of three (3) EMJM programs per application cycle9. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Bachelor's degree holders or final-year undergraduate students 
graduating prior to program start10. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: No 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: No 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: No 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes (if graduating before Master's intake)10. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: Yes10. 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes9. 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: October / November annually9. 
●​ Closing Date: January / February 12 (varies per consortium)9. 
●​ Application Cycle: Annual3. 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2026–202713. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: 100% full coverage of participation costs, tuition, and enrollment fees10. 
●​ Monthly Stipend: €1,400 per month living allowance (up to 24 months maximum)3. 
●​ Allowance: Travel, visa, and installation contributions integrated into overall grant10. 
●​ Book Allowance: Integrated into monthly living stipend10. 
●​ Transportation: Travel cost allowance included10. 
●​ Insurance: Comprehensive health and accident insurance coverage10. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Research Allowance: Research and thesis preparation costs included10. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Return Service: None3. 
●​ Other Benefits: Award of a joint degree or multiple degrees upon completion10. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Bachelor's Diploma or Official Certificate of Expected Graduation10. 
2.​ Official Academic Transcripts of Records (TOR)10. 
3.​ Proof of English Language Proficiency (IELTS / TOEFL)9. 
4.​ Motivation Letter / Statement of Purpose9. 

5.​ Two Academic / Professional Recommendation Letters9. 
6.​ Passport / Proof of Nationality9. 
7.​ Curriculum Vitae (Europass format)9. 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Satisfy academic progression standards established by consortium 
regulations11. 
●​ Regular Load: Full credit load per semester11. 
●​ No Failures: Pass all mandatory study modules11. 
●​ Return Service: None3. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Failure to complete Bachelor's degree prior to Master's program commencement10. 
●​ Applying to more than three Erasmus Mundus Joint Master programs in a single cycle9. 
●​ Non-compliance with compulsory physical mobility track rules11.

Strategic Recommendations for ISKONNECT 
Architecture 
The integration of these international and bilateral scholarship parameters requires specific 
enhancements to the ISKONNECT multi-stage recommendation pipeline1. 
First, professional work experience validation must be added as a mandatory Stage 2 query 
constraint1. Programs such as Australia Awards (ID 74), Fulbright-PhilSA (ID 90), and Chevening 
(ID 91) strictly require at least two years of post-graduation work experience24. Without 
validating user.work_experience_years >= 2, undergraduate seniors and fresh graduates will 
receive false-positive recommendations, leading to immediate disqualification during agency 
eligibility screening1. 
Second, foreign GPA conversion routines must be standardized within the recommendation 
logic1. Global Korea Scholarship tracks (IDs 64 & 65) mandate a minimum cumulative grade 
point average of 80% or placement in the top 20% of the class19. The engine must normalize 
Philippine General Weighted Average (GWA) values—where 1.0 represents the highest score 
and 3.0 represents passing—into standard NIIED percentile equivalents to prevent 
false-negative omissions of eligible top-tier Filipino students1. 
Third, temporal age filters must be calculated relative to fixed intake benchmark dates rather 
than real-time application submission dates1. MEXT tracks (IDs 81, 82, 83) set strict age cutoffs 
based on birthdates on or after April 2, 200229. Similarly, GKS Undergraduate (ID 65) requires 
candidates to be under 25 years of age as of the intake year18. Evaluating user birthdates 
dynamically against these intake thresholds ensures exact temporal eligibility alignment across 
the platform1.
