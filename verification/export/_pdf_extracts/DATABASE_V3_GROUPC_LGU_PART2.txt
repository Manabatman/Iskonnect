Canonical Verification Report for 
ISKONNECT Scholarship Database 
This canonical verification report establishes the definitive ground truth for sixteen local 
government unit (LGU) and specialized scholarship programs within the ISKONNECT 
scholarship matching platform1. To prevent false-positive recommendations that direct 
applicants toward ineligible grants and false-negative omissions that conceal viable funding, 
each scholarship record has been audited against Tier 1 primary evidence, including 
government portals, official ordinances, downloadable citizen charters, and governing 
executive orders2. 
Per ISKONNECT verification protocol, fields not explicitly declared in primary source 
documentation are designated as NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE to preserve 
deterministic database mapping2. 
Pasig City Regular Academic Scholarship (ID: 25) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: Pasig City Regular Academic Scholarship Program4 
●​ Provider: Pasig City Government (Pasig City Education Department – Scholarships and 
Awards Section / PCED-SAS)5 
●​ Category: Local Government Unit / Merit-and-Need1 
●​ Official Website: https://www.pasigcity.gov.ph1 
●​ Application Portal: https://scholars.pasigcity.gov.ph1 
●​ Official Guidelines: Pasig City Education Department Citizen's Charter5 
●​ Current Status: Active1 
2. Purpose 
The Pasig City Regular Academic Scholarship Program expands educational access for 
bonafide Pasig City residents across basic and tertiary education levels5. By offering semestral 
financial grants to students from low-to-middle-income families, the program seeks to alleviate 
household financial pressure while encouraging high academic achievement in public and 
private institutions5.

●​ Education Level: Elementary, Junior High School, Senior High School, College4 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: All year levels (Grade 1 through College Senior)1 

●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No7 
●​ Existing College Students?: Yes7 
●​ Graduate Students?: No1

institution4 
●​ Academic Requirements: Passing general weighted average as certified by report of 
grades5 
●​ Minimum GWA: 85.00%1 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Income Ceilings: Combined family gross annual income not exceeding PHP 300,000.00 
or submission of a Barangay Certificate of Indigence1 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ School Restrictions: Recognized public or private schools5 
●​ Priority Courses: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Good Moral: Certificate of Good Moral Character8 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE 
●​ Other Official Rules: Strict enforcement of the "One-Scholar-One-Family Policy" (only
