Executive Verification Protocol & Architecture 
The ISKONNECT scholarship matching platform operates an automated engine designed to 
process student profile vectors against financial assistance programs across public, institutional, 
and private sectors. To eliminate false-positive recommendations that direct applicants to 
ineligible programs and false-negative omissions that suppress viable funding, this report 
establishes the canonical verification ground truth for all database records that have not yet 
undergone canonical auditing. 
Enterprise database updates require deterministic verification: every eligibility parameter must 
be validated against Tier 1 primary evidence, mapped to explicit SQL data types, and reconciled 
with governing agency mandates. Where an official provider document or governing policy does 
not explicitly state a requirement, platform protocol mandates designating the field as NOT 
SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE rather than introducing unverified assumptions. 
Tier 1: Official Sources (.gov.ph, Official Portals, Downloadable 
Guidelines, CMOs/PPGs)​
  └── Tier 2: Official Announcements (Press Releases, FAQs, Official 
Social Advisories)​
        └── Tier 3: Trusted Secondary Sources (PhilScholar, University 
Scholarship Offices)​
              └── Tier 4: Unverified Aggregators & Forums [DISCARDED]​
 
Data verification strictly follows a four-tiered authority hierarchy: 
●​ Tier 1 (Highest Authority): Direct government agency web portals (.gov.ph), official 
provider portals, downloadable policy guidelines, CHED Memorandum Orders (CMOs), 
GSIS Policy and Procedural Guidelines (PPGs), DOST Administrative Orders, and official 
application portals. 
●​ Tier 2 (Official Announcements): Press releases, downloadable application instructions, 
official FAQs, and direct advisories published by scholarship secretariats. 
●​ Tier 3 (Trusted Secondary Sources): University scholarship office notices and 
recognized educational registries used exclusively to locate primary documentation. Tier 3 
data cannot override Tier 1 or Tier 2 mandates. 
●​ Tier 4 (Unverified Sources): Blogs, student forums, social media aggregators, and 
unverified AI summaries. These sources are permanently excluded from live database 
updates.

Program Audit 1: CHED Medical Scholarship and Return Service 
(MSRS) Program 

1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: CHED Medical Scholarship and Return Service (MSRS) Program 
(enacted under Republic Act No. 11509, "Doktor Para sa Bayan Act"). 
●​ Provider: Commission on Higher Education (CHED) in joint partnership with the 
Department of Health (DOH). 
●​ Category: Government / National / Graduate / Merit-and-Need / Return Service 
Obligation. 
●​ Official Website: https://ched.gov.ph/msrs/ 
●​ Official Application Portal: Integrated via accredited partner State Universities and 
Colleges (SUCs) and Partner Private Higher Education Institutions (PHEIs). 
●​ Official Downloadable Guidelines: CHED Memorandum Order (CMO) No. 10, s. 2026 
("Guidelines for the Medical Scholarship and Return Service Program"); CHED 
Administrative Order (CAO) No. 2, s. 2022. 
●​ Official Announcement: Office of the Chairperson Memorandum: Call for Applications 
for First Semester AY 2025–2026. 
●​ Current Status: Active. 
●​ Last Verified Date: 2026-08-05. 
2. Purpose 
To expand access to medical education for deserving Filipino students and address the critical 
shortage of health human resources in underserved, remote, and low-income municipalities by 
establishing a mandatory return service framework in public health facilities. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Natural-born or naturalized Filipino citizen residing in the Philippines. 
●​ Residency: Must reside in the Philippines, with priority given to applicants residing in 
municipalities without government physicians, Geographically Isolated and 
Disadvantaged Areas (GIDA), top 20% poorest provinces, calamity/conflict zones, or 
low-income municipalities with high poverty incidence. 
●​ Education Level: Graduate (Doctor of Medicine degree program). 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Incoming 1st Year medical students, as well as regular ongoing 2nd 
Year, 3rd Year, and 4th Year medical students. 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Eligible (as medical students in years 1 through 4). 
●​ Graduate Students?: Yes (Doctor of Medicine is a post-baccalaureate degree). 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must be accepted or enrolled in a Doctor of Medicine 
program at an accredited partner SUC or PHEI. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Must have completed a prerequisite Bachelor's degree; 
obtained a National Medical Admission Test (NMAT) score mandated by CHED and 
required by the partner HEI; passed school admission standards. 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Governed by partner HEI 
admission and retention standards). 
●​ Alternative Class Rank Requirements: Priority ranking applied based on GIDA status, 
municipal doctor coverage, and socioeconomic standing. 
●​ Income Ceilings: Combined annual gross family income must not exceed PHP 

450,000.00. 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted to participating SUCs or designated Partner PHEIs in 
regions lacking SUC medical programs. 
●​ Partner School Requirements: Partner PHEIs must provide counterpart funds to cover 
tuition balances exceeding the CHED ceiling. 
●​ Priority Courses: Doctor of Medicine (including direct entrants of integrated liberal arts 
and medicine programs such as INTARMED after completing initial 2 years). 
●​ Province / Region Restrictions: Priority distributed per region based on DOH health 
worker population projections. 
●​ Gender Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ PWD / IP Requirements: Priority given to IP/ethnic group members (certified by 
NCIP/LGU) and dependents of community health volunteers. 
●​ Good Moral: Required (Certificate of Good Moral Character). 
●​ Health Requirements: Physically and mentally fit to undergo medical education. 
●​ Other Official Eligibility Rules: Applicants carrying existing scholarship grants with 
mandatory return service obligations are ineligible. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Incoming 1st Year medical students and ongoing 2nd, 3rd, and 4th Year 
medical students. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes (as incoming 1st year medical students). 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes (4th year medical students). * Can graduates apply?: 
Yes (Bachelor's degree graduates entering medical school). 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes, provided they have no active overlapping 
scholarship obligations. 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: Announced per AY cycle during the summer period preceding the 
academic year. 
●​ Closing Date: Set annually (e.g., extended to November 11 for AY 2025–2026). 
●​ Application Cycle: Annual (with semestral disbursement verification). 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–2027. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: 100% TOSF covered in SUCs. For Partner PHEIs, CHED provides up to PHP 
100,000.00 per semester, with PHEI counterpart funds covering any remaining excess. * 
Monthly Stipend: Integrated into living subsidy (PHP 5,000.00/month = PHP 
30,000.00/semester). 
●​ Allowance: Lodging allowance (PHP 5,000.00/month = PHP 30,000.00/semester). 
●​ Book Allowance: PHP 13,000.00 per semester (PHP 
26,0[span_63](start_span)[span_63](end_span)[span_66](start_span)[span_66](end_spa
n)00.00 per AY). 

●​ Transportation: PHP 1,000.00/month = PHP 6,000.00 per semester (PHP 12,000.00 per 
AY). 
●​ Internet / Connectivity: Integrated into Miscellaneous Allowance (PHP 6,000.00 per 
semester). 
●​ Uniform: PHP 
3[span_137](start_span)[span_137](end_span)[span_149](start_span)[span_149](end_sp
an),500.00 per semester (PHP 7,000.00 per AY). 
●​ Insurance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Board Exam Subsidy: One-time licensure review and application grant of PHP 
16,000.00. 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Employment Opportunities: Direct integration into the DOH Doctor to the Barrios 
(DTTB) program or public health facilities. 
●​ Return Service: Mandatory 1 year of service in public health offices or government 
hospitals for every 1 year of scholarship availed. 
●​ Additional Benefits: Internship support during Post-Graduate Internship (PGI) year. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Proof of Citizenship (Birth Certificate / PhilSys ID). 
2.​ Certificate of Good Moral Character. 
3.​ Certification of Acceptance / Registration Form from Partner SUC/PHEI. 
4.​ Certified True Copy of Bachelor's Degree Transcript of Records (TOR) or NMAT Score 
Report. 
5.​ Proof of Income (ITR / BIR Tax Exemption Certificate not exceeding PHP 450,000). 
6.​ Affidavit of No Existing Scholarship Grant. 
7.​ Signed Scholar's Commitment to Render Return Service Obligation. 
8.​ Priority Group Certifications (GIDA residency, NCIP IP certificate, MHO Health Volunteer 
dependent certificate). 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Comply with the academic requirements and retention policies of the 
school. 
●​ Regular Load: Enrolled in full-time medical curriculum per semester. 
●​ No Failures: No failing grades in medical subjects as governed by HEI retention rules. 
●​ Attendance: Full compliance with medical school attendance policies. 
●​ Volunteer Work: Summer Immersion Program (SIP) or Community Orientation Summer 
Workshop (COSW). 
●​ Return Service: Execution of notarized scholarship contract binding scholar to return 
service. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Combined family gross annual income exceeding PHP 450,000.00. 
●​ Enjoyment of another active scholarship grant carrying return service obligations. 
●​ Academic failure or dismissal from the partner HEI Doctor of Medicine program. 

●​ Failure or refusal to sign the mandatory return service contract.

2. Purpose 
To provide targeted financial subsidies to children of low-income GSIS members pursuing 
undergraduate STEM degree programs in CHED-accredited autonomous or deregulated 
institutions. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Filipino Citizen. 
●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Education Level: Undergraduate / College. 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Year 1 (Incoming Freshmen).

●​ Existing College Students?: Ineligible for initial application. 
●​ Graduate Students?: Ineligible. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Accepted or enrolled in an eligible STEM Bachelor's 
degree program. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Senior High School (SHS) overall GWA of at least 90.00% 
with zero failing grades. 
●​ Minimum GWA: 90.00% (SHS entry cutoff). 
●​ Alternative Class Rank Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Income Ceilings: Controlled via parent employment status: Member must be at Salary 
Grade 15 or below (or equivalent). 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ School Restrictions: CHED-registered Autonomous and Deregulated Private Higher 
Education Institutions or recognized State/Local Universities and Colleges. 
●​ Partner School Requirements: Autonomous or Deregulated CHED status required. 
●​ Priority Courses: GSIS-identified STEM Bachelor's Degree Courses. 
●​ Province / Region Restrictions: Slots distributed proportionately per region based on 
GSIS membership. 
●​ Gender Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ PWD / IP Requirements: At least 20% of slots per region reserved for PWDs, IPs, and 
Solo Parents. 
●​ Good Moral: Required. 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Other Official Eligibility Rules: Member parent must have updated premium payments 
and permanent employment status. 
4. Application Timing

●​ Can current sophomores apply?: No.

Multi-Stage Engine Matching Pipeline Architecture 
To enforce these canonical updates, the live matching engine must route user profile vectors 
through a strict three-stage pipeline: 
1.​ Stage 1: Hard Level & Temporal Filtering 
○​ Verifies whether user.year_level is contained within 
scholarship.eligible_year_levels. 
○​ Enforces user.earned_college_units == 0 if scholarship.incoming_year_only is set 
to true. 
○​ Validates user.is_currently_enrolled against 
scholarship.requires_current_enrollment. 
2.​ Stage 2: Quantitative & Socioeconomic Eligibility Bounds 
○​ Evaluates user.gwa >= scholarship.minimum_gwa. 
○​ Confirms user.family_income <= scholarship.income_limit. 
○​ Checks user.parent_salary_grade <= 15 for GSIS GSSP (ID 78). 
3.​ Stage 3: Institutional, Sectoral & Course Validation 
○​ Matches user.course_code against scholarship.degree_program_restricted. 
○​ Verifies user.sectoral_status (e.g., NCFRS registration for CoScho ID 117). 
○​ Enforces LGU residency and non-enjoyment of other LGU grants (e.g., QCitizen ID 
for QCSP ID 88). 
Executing these database updates and pipeline enhancements aligns the live ISKONNECT 
database with canonical provider rules, eliminating false recommendations and providing 
Filipino students with reliable scholarship matching results.
