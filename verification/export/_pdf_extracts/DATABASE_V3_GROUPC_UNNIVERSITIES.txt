UP College of Law Scholarship Program (ID: 70) 
1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Name: UP College of Law Scholarship Program17 
●​ Provider: UP College of Law (University of the Philippines Diliman)17 
●​ Category: Institutional / Graduate / Professional Law / Need-and-Equity3 
●​ Official Website: 
https://law.upd.edu.ph/call-for-scholarship-applications-ay-2025-2026/17 
●​ Application Portal: Integrated UP Law Online Scholarship Application System17 
●​ Official Guidelines: UP College of Law Scholarship Announcement and Guidelines17 
●​ Current Status: Active3 
2. Purpose 
To uphold access, diversity, and equity in legal education by supporting Juris Doctor students 
facing economic or personal hardship, providing full financial coverage from Law Aptitude 
Exam (LAE) fee waivers through Bar Examination preparation17. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Natural-born or naturalized Filipino citizen12. 
●​ Residency: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Education Level: Graduate (Juris Doctor Program)3. 
●​ Eligible Year Levels: Years 1, 2, 3, and 4 (Juris Doctor)3. 
●​ Incoming Freshman Only?: No17. 
●​ Existing College Students?: Ineligible (Restricted to law students)17. 
●​ Graduate Students?: Yes (Juris Doctor professional program)17. 
●​ Current Enrollment Requirement: Must be a bona fide Juris Doctor student at UP College 
of Law17. 
●​ Academic Requirements: Need is the primary selection criterion; academic merit is 
secondary17. 
●​ Minimum GWA: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE (Evaluated holistically based on 

hardship)17. 
●​ Alternative Class Rank: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Income Ceilings: Evaluated based on Income Tax Return and household asset verification 
form17. 
●​ Age Restrictions: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ School Restrictions: Restricted strictly to UP College of Law (Diliman and BGC 
campuses)17. 
●​ Priority Courses: Juris Doctor (JD) degree program17. 
●​ Sectoral Requirements: Diversity considerations explicitly prioritized: LGBTQIA+ 
individuals, members of Indigenous Peoples (IP) communities, Persons with Disabilities 
(PWDs), single parents, and aspiring first-generation lawyers17. 
●​ Good Moral: Required17. 
●​ Health Requirements: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Other Official Rules: Applicants must forego other active scholarship awards upon 
acceptance to UP Law Scholarship17. Background checks and home asset inspections are 
conducted17. 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: All bona fide Juris Doctor students at UP College of Law17. 
●​ Can current freshmen apply?: Yes (1st year JD students)17. 
●​ Can current sophomores apply?: Yes (2nd year JD students)17. 
●​ Can current juniors apply?: Yes (3rd year JD students)17. 
●​ Can current seniors apply?: Yes (4th year JD students)17. 
●​ Can graduates apply?: Yes (LAE applicants receiving application fee waivers)17. 
●​ Can previous applicants reapply?: Yes17. 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: June 30 annually17. 
●​ Closing Date: July 20 annually (Deadlines strictly applied)17. 
●​ Application Cycle: Annual. 
●​ Current AY Covered: AY 2025–2026 / AY 2026–202717. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition: 100% waiver of tuition and miscellaneous fees17. 
●​ Monthly Stipend: PHP 10,000.00 per month for 3 to 4 months per semester (Tier 1 & Tier 
2 scholars)17. 
●​ Allowance: Internet and book allowance set at PHP 10,000.00 per semester17. 
●​ Book Allowance: Included in semestral internet/book allowance package17. 
●​ Transportation: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Insurance: Access to UP Health Service benefits16. 

●​ Board Exam Subsidy: Full support for Bar Examination preparation and review fees17. 
●​ Research Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: NOT SPECIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE. 
●​ Return Service: Scholars render student service assistance to the UP College of Law17. 
●​ Other Benefits: LAE application fee waiver; disbursement directly via Landbank 
account17. 
7. Required Documents 
1.​ Accomplished UP Law Online Scholarship Application Form17. 
2.​ Letter of Intent detailing financial hardship, family dynamics, and personal 
circumstances17. 
3.​ Latest Income Tax Return (ITR) of applicant, spouse, parents, or supporting siblings17. 
4.​ Household Asset Photos (front of home, living room, kitchen, bedroom, and major 
appliances)17. 
5.​ Landbank Account Details for stipend disbursement17. 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Maintain GWA: Maintain good academic standing per UP Law academic retention rules17. 
●​ Regular Load: Full-time credit enrollment in Juris Doctor curriculum17. 
●​ No Failures: Compliance with law deanship retention rules17. 
●​ Return Service: Render student assistant service hours to the UP College of Law17. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Holding another active scholarship award without submitting an official withdrawal 
letter17. 
●​ Misrepresentation during household asset inspection or background check17. 
●​ Failure to submit all required ITRs/photos before the strict July 20 deadline17.
