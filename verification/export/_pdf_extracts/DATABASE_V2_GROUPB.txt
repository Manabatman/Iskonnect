Executive Verification Protocol & System Architecture 
The ISKONNECT scholarship platform operates an automated matching engine designed to 
connect Filipino students with higher education financial assistance. To eliminate false-positive 
recommendations that misdirect applicants and false-negative omissions that hide viable 
funding, this verification report establishes the canonical ground truth for production database 
updates. In a live scholarship matching system, traditional document summarization is 
inadequate. Summarization retains qualitative ambiguities, misses edge-case constraints, and 
yields loose data types. Production database updates require deterministic validation: every 
eligibility parameter must be validated against primary evidence, mapped to strict SQL column 
data types, and verified against governing policy mandates. If an official governing body or 
foundation document does not explicitly state a requirement, the platform standard demands 
that the field be designated as Not specified in official source, rather than relying on unverified 
assumptions.

Data verification follows a strict four-tiered authority hierarchy: 
●​ Tier 1 — Official Sources (Highest Authority): Direct government agency web portals 
(.gov.ph), official scholarship provider portals, official downloadable PDFs/guidelines, 
CHED Memoranda, DOST Administrative Orders, and verified Facebook pages managed 
directly by scholarship secretariats. Tier 1 documents represent the immutable source of 
truth. 
●​ Tier 2 — Official Announcements: Press releases, downloadable application 
instructions, official FAQs, admission notices, and formal advisories published directly by 
governing bodies. 
●​ Tier 3 — Trusted Secondary Sources: Reputable aggregators such as PhilScholar, 
university scholarship office notices, and recognized educational organizations. 

Secondary sources serve strictly to clarify or locate primary documentation and can never 
override Tier 1 or Tier 2 official data. If PhilScholar conflicts with an official agency 
mandate (e.g., DOST, CHED, SM Foundation, Megaworld Foundation), the official 
provider rule strictly supersedes. 
●​ Tier 4 — Unverified Sources: Blogs, AI-generated summaries, discussion forums, and 
secondary aggregators lacking primary citations. These sources are completely excluded 
from production database updates.

1. Scholarship Identity 
●​ Official Scholarship Name: DOST-SEI Science & Technology Undergraduate 
Scholarships (Republic Act No. 7687 & Merit Scholarship Tracks). 
●​ Provider: Department of Science and Technology – Science Education Institute 
(DOST-SEI). 
●​ Program Category: Government / National / Merit-and-Need. 
●​ Official Websites: https://www.science-scholarships.ph,

●​ Scholarship Status: Active. 
2. Purpose 
To stimulate and entice talented Filipino youth to pursue lifetime productive careers in science 
and technology, ensuring a steady and adequate supply of qualified S&T human resources to 
steer national progress. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Must be a natural-born Filipino citizen. 
●​ Residency: For RA 7687, must be a resident of the municipality for at least four (4) years 
as attested by the barangay. For Merit, no municipal duration constraint is specified. 
●​ Education Level: Senior High School (Grade 12) graduating students or High School / 
SHS prior graduates who have not entered college. 

●​ Enrollment Status: Unenrolled in college; must have earned zero (0) post-secondary, 
college, or vocational units. 
●​ Academic Performance & Strand Rules: 
○​ STEM Strand: All graduating Grade 12 STEM students are eligible regardless of 
class rank. 
○​ Non-STEM Strand: Must belong to the top 5% of the Grade 11 graduating/ranking 
class. 
○​ Prior SHS/HS Graduates: STEM graduates eligible; Non-STEM graduates must 
belong to top 5% of their graduating class. 
●​ Socio-Economic Criteria: 
○​ RA 7687 Track: Family socio-economic status must not exceed the set cut-off 
values/poverty threshold limit. 
○​ Merit Track: Awarded to students with high aptitude in science/math whose family 
income exceeds the poverty threshold. 
●​ Health & Character: Must be of good moral character and in good health. 
●​ Institution & Course Restrictions: Must enroll in a CHED-recognized Center of 
Excellence (COE) / Center of Development (COD), or FAAP Level III accredited S&T 
priority program, or at the University of the Philippines / State Universities and Colleges 
(SUCs). 
4. Application Timing 
●​ *Who May Apply*: Graduating Grade 12 students for the current academic year, and 
previous SHS/HS graduates who have never enrolled in college. 
●​ Can an existing first-year college student newly apply?: No. 
●​ Can an existing second-year college student newly apply?: No (Must apply via JLSS 
instead). 
●​ Can an existing third-year college student newly apply?: No. 
●​ Can an existing fourth-year college student newly apply?: No. 
●​ Can someone who already graduated apply?: No. 
●​ Can someone who already missed the intended intake apply?: No (Once college 
units are earned, freshman track eligibility is permanently forfeited). 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening Date: October 20, 2025. 
●​ Closing Date: November 21, 2025. 
●​ Qualifying Examination Date: February 21–22, 2026. 
●​ Academic Year Covered: AY 2026–2027. 
●​ Cycle Type: Fixed / Annual. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition Subsidy: Up to ₱40,000.00 per academic year for private HEIs (₱10,000.00 for 
midyear). 
●​ Monthly Stipend: ₱8,000.00 per month (₱96,000.00 per 10-month academic year). 
●​ Learning Materials / Connectivity Allowance: ₱10,000.00 per academic year 
(₱5,000.00 per semester). 

●​ Clothing Allowance: ₱1,000.00 initial one-time allowance for first-year scholars. 
●​ Transportation Allowance: Provided for scholars studying outside their home province. 
●​ Group Insurance: Full group health and accident insurance coverage. 
●​ Thesis Allowance: ₱10,000.00 outright grant. 
●​ Graduation Allowance: ₱1,000.00. 
7. Required Documents 
●​ PSA Birth Certificate. 
●​ Form 138 / SF9 (Learner's Progress Report Card). 
●​ Form C — Certificate of Good Moral Character. 
●​ Form D — Certificate of Good Health. 
●​ Form F — Certificate of Residency (for RA 7687). 
●​ Parent/Guardian Proof of Income (ITR or BIR Certificate of Tax Exemption). 
●​ Form G — Commitment to Return of Service. 
●​ Form H — Applicant's Certification & Form I — Signed Declaration. 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Must maintain a passing General Weighted Average (GWA) specified by DOST-SEI rules 
each term. 
●​ Must carry a full academic load per semester as prescribed in the priority S&T curriculum. 
●​ Must have no failing, incomplete, or dropped grades in any academic subject. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Enrollment in college prior to application or possession of earned tertiary/vocational units. 
●​ Failure to pass the national DOST-SEI Pen-and-Paper Qualifying Examination. 
●​ Dual enjoyment of major government funding covering the same expenditure items. 
●​ Non-natural-born citizenship. 
10. Temporal Eligibility 
●​ Current Grade 12 Student (STEM): Eligible Now. 
●​ Current Grade 12 Student (Non-STEM, Top 5%): Eligible Now. 
●​ Current Grade 11 Student: Eligible Later (Must wait for Grade 12 intake cycle). 
●​ Enrolled 1st-Year College Student: No Longer Eligible for Undergraduate Track (Eligible 
Later for JLSS in 2nd Year). 
●​ Enrolled 2nd-Year College Student: No Longer Eligible for Undergraduate Track 
(Eligible Now for JLSS).

2. Purpose 
To support academically talented and deserving Filipino students entering college by providing 

merit-based financial assistance for priority undergraduate programs. 
3. Official Eligibility Requirements 
●​ Citizenship: Filipino Citizen. 
●​ Entry Status: Incoming first-year college student. Must have earned zero (0) units in 
tertiary education. 
●​ Academic Eligibility Cutoff: 
○​ BPMSP Track: Must belong to the Top Five (5) of the graduating SHS class OR 
have a minimum Grade 12 GWA of 95% (or equivalent). 
○​ CMSP Track: Minimum Grade 12 GWA of 93% or equivalent. 
●​ Parental Income Ceiling: 
○​ BPMSP: Combined annual gross income of parents/guardians must not exceed 
₱2,000,000.00. 
○​ CMSP: Combined annual gross income must not exceed ₱500,000.00. 
●​ Age Requirement: No age limit is imposed. 
●​ Old Curriculum Graduates: Fourth-year high school graduates from SY 2015–2016 or 
earlier are eligible provided they never enrolled in tertiary education. 
●​ TESDA NC Holders: TESDA National Certificate holders are eligible for 1st-year entry if 
they have earned no college units. 
●​ Institutional Placement: Must be enrolled in a CHED-identified priority program offered 
by a DHEI (Delivering HEI with COE/COD status, Level III-IV program accreditation, or 
SUC/LUC with Institutional Recognition). 
4. Application Timing 
●​ Who May Apply: Graduating SHS Grade 12 students, prior SHS/HS graduates with no 
tertiary units, and incoming 1st-year college students. 
●​ Can an existing first-year student newly apply?: Yes, provided they are in their initial 
intake term and have not credited/earned upper-level units. 
●​ Can transferees or shiftees apply?: No ("Applicants who are transferees or shiftees 
with credited units... are ineligible"). 
5. Application Window 
●​ Opening / Closing Dates: Announced annually via CHED Regional Offices. 
●​ Cycle Type: Fixed / Annual. 
6. Benefits 
●​ Tuition & Other School Fees (TOSF): Up to ₱45,000.00 per semester (₱90,000.00 per 
academic year). Excess TOSF above ₱45,000 per sem must be covered by the scholar. 
●​ Stipends & Incentives: Includes Academic Excellence Incentives for top-ranked 
qualifiers. 
7. Required Documents 
●​ Accomplished Online Application Form (Annex A). 

●​ PSA Birth Certificate. 
●​ Certified True Copy of Form 138 / SF9 (Grade 12 Report Card). 
●​ Certificate of Class Rank (Top 5 certification signed by Principal/Registrar, if GWA < 95%). 
●​ Proof of Income: BIR Form 1701 / 2316, BIR Certificate of Tax Exemption / Non-Filer, or 
DSWD 4Ps Certification. 
8. Renewal Requirements 
●​ Must maintain required academic GWA per semester without failing grades. 
●​ Continuous full-time enrollment in designated priority programs. 
9. Disqualifying Conditions 
●​ Earning tertiary units prior to scholarship award. 
●​ Transferees or shiftees carrying credited tertiary units. 
●​ Combined parental gross income exceeding ₱2,000,000.00 for BPMSP or ₱500,000.00 
for CMSP. 
10. Temporal Eligibility 
●​ Incoming 1st-Year Student (GWA \ge 95\% or Top 5): Eligible Now. 
●​ Current 2nd/3rd-Year Student: No Longer Eligible.
